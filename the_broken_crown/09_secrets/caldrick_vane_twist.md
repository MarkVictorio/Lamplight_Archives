# Secret: Ser Caldrick Vane is a vampire

**Spoilers:** Player-facing lore for **Elliot** and the Argent Veil is wrong about Caldrick’s nature.

**Related:** [Caldrick Vane (full GM dossier)](../03_npcs/characters/caldrick_vane.md) · [Elliot — Argent Veil background](../07_player_characters/elliot_argent_veil_background.md) (what PCs are meant to believe)

---

## The twist

**Ser Caldrick Vane** — the **Veiled Champion**, slayer of the Elder Lycan of Aaldharyn, living symbol of the Argent Veil — is a **vampire** (**Newblood**), not merely a long-lived elf.

Everything that “explains” his centuries of service and his never-seen face under the helm is **compatible with that lie**: elven longevity, discipline, mystique of the order. The truth reframes him as an **active, dangerous** figure in the **return of vampirism**, tied to **Nerscylla** and **Vaelroth (the Pale Ascendant)** and to the wider **post-Crown occult resurgence**.

---

## Why it lands

- **Elliot** was trained and patronized by Caldrick; the mentor is the monster (or the tragic victim — or both).  
- The party may have **seen the statues** and heard the **Hero of Aaldharyn** story as gospel.  
- Vampire sealing lore in Elliot’s own studies (e.g. **Rite of Crimson Silence**) gains irony if his master is what those rites were written to fight or contain.

---

## Deployment notes

- Decide **when** the mask slips: hunger, holy ground, a record that contradicts “elf,” a witness from Aaldharyn, or Nerscylla/Vaelroth plotlines.  
- Preserve **moral ambiguity**: Caldrick can remain a **teacher** worth mourning even after the reveal.  
- Do not assume players read this repo — treat this file as **GM reference** only.
