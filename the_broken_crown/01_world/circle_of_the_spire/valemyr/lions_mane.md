# Lion’s Mane — House Valemyr

**Role:** Knowledge, Archives, Memory

**Master lore:** [Aaldharyn_World_Lore.md](../../Aaldharyn_World_Lore.md) (Lion’s Mane) · **Faction:** [Circle of the Spire](../../../02_factions/Circle%20of%20the%20Spire.md#house-valemyr)

---

Lion’s Mane is the stronghold of House Valemyr, surrounded by dense forests and protected knowledge vaults.

It is the greatest repository of recorded history in the kingdom.

---

## Hidden Truth

Lion’s Mane is a **memory anchor**.

It preserves not only written history, but fragments of truths the world itself has forgotten.
