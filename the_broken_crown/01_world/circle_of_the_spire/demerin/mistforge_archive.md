# Mistforge Archive

**Master lore:** [Aaldharyn_World_Lore.md](../../Aaldharyn_World_Lore.md) (Mistforge Archive) · **Faction:** [Circle of the Spire](../../../02_factions/Circle%20of%20the%20Spire.md#house-demerin)

---

A remote archive structure separated from the mainland.

Access to the Mistforge Archive is tightly controlled through designated routes and conditions.

These access points are overseen and regulated by House Demerin.

---

## Implication

Demerin does not only control recorded history.

They control who is allowed to reach knowledge that has not yet been altered.
