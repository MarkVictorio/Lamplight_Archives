# Achroma — House Demerin

**Role:** Historical Consistency, Mediation

**Master lore:** [Aaldharyn_World_Lore.md](../../Aaldharyn_World_Lore.md) (Achroma) · **Faction:** [Circle of the Spire](../../../02_factions/Circle%20of%20the%20Spire.md#house-demerin)

---

Achroma is a stark and controlled city where records are validated and histories are aligned.

It ensures that the kingdom shares a single version of truth.

---

## Hidden Truth

Achroma does not preserve truth.

It preserves **stability**.

Contradictions are removed, rewritten, or forgotten.

---

## Tension with Valemyr

Valemyr’s [Argent Vale](../valemyr/argent_vale.md) studies contradictions in records; Demerin’s mandate is to reconcile—or erase—them. See *Monastic Culture* in the Argent Vale file for the in-world friction.
