# Sunstone — House Ashcombe

**Role:** Legacy, Ritual, Bloodline

**Master lore:** [Aaldharyn_World_Lore.md](../../Aaldharyn_World_Lore.md) (Sunstone) · **Faction:** [Circle of the Spire](../../../02_factions/Circle%20of%20the%20Spire.md#house-ashcombe)

---

Sunstone serves as the seat of House Ashcombe, whose lineage traces back to the era of the Crown.

The estate is elegant, but its foundations are far older than its current structure.

---

## Political Position

House Ashcombe is the weakest of the five houses in open power.

* Limited military strength
* Reliant on historical legitimacy rather than force
* Often sidelined in major decisions of the Circle

Despite this, they maintain influence through what they **know** rather than what they command.

Ashcombe operates carefully, rarely confronting other houses directly, but constantly seeking opportunities to regain relevance.

---

## Hidden Truth

Sunstone is a **ritual site**.

Its design and placement suggest it was used to activate or interact with the forces that created Thralls.

---

## Underlying Drive

Ashcombe does not seek stability.

They seek **ascension**.

They believe their bloodline entitles them to something greater than their current position—and they are willing to risk the balance of the kingdom to reclaim it.
