# Soulstone — House Greifenhald

**Role:** Military Power, Containment

**Master lore:** [Aaldharyn_World_Lore.md](../../Aaldharyn_World_Lore.md) (Soulstone) · **Faction:** [Circle of the Spire](../../../02_factions/Circle%20of%20the%20Spire.md#house-greifenhald)

---

Soulstone is a heavily fortified stronghold commanding the southern regions.

It serves as the backbone of the kingdom’s military might.

---

## Political Position

House Greifenhald holds the greatest capacity for open force in the kingdom.

* Controls standing armies
* Maintains fortifications and supply lines
* Acts as the kingdom’s shield against external threats

They value strength, clarity, and decisive action.

However, their authority depends on the legitimacy upheld by the Circle.

---

## Tension with House Ashcombe

Soulstone sits at the center of a growing tension between Greifenhald and Ashcombe.

* Greifenhald views Ashcombe as weak and politically irrelevant
* Ashcombe views Greifenhald as a blunt instrument unworthy of ruling

Recent movements suggest Ashcombe has taken interest in regions under Greifenhald’s influence.

This has led to:

* Increased military presence around shared borders
* Quiet surveillance of Ashcombe agents
* Unspoken readiness for conflict

Greifenhald does not trust Ashcombe’s connection to the Crown.

Ashcombe resents Greifenhald’s dominance.

---

## Hidden Truth

The land beneath Soulstone is saturated with death.

It functions as a **containment zone**, holding something that should not move.

---

## Underlying Risk

If Ashcombe succeeds in awakening or controlling what lies beneath the kingdom,

Soulstone will be the first line of defense.

And possibly the first to fall.

---

## Soul Cairn

The largest graveyard in the southern region. **Demerin researchers** have established a presence on site (at least a month planned as of [Session 8](../../06_sessions/session_08.md)).

From play:

- **Two additional armories** exist within Soul Cairn (beyond sites already encountered).
- **Festival of the Dead** — relatively young festival (~30–40 years); displays relics tied to the **Last Vampire** ([Session 8](../../06_sessions/session_08.md)).
- Coffins emptied from Soul Cairn were found in the hidden Sunstone ritual chamber ([Session 7](../../06_sessions/session_07.md)).
