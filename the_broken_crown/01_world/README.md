## Layout convention

* **`circle_of_the_spire/<house>/`** — Great Holds and primary sites under that house’s control (mirrors the structure described in [Circle of the Spire](../02_factions/Circle%20of%20the%20Spire.md)).
* **This directory’s root** — religion docs, world overview, and regions without a single-house anchor.
