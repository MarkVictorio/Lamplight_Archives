# The Ascended Pantheon

The dominant religions of the kingdom revolve around a group of beings known collectively as **The Ascended**.

According to surviving traditions, these figures were **once mortal individuals who transcended the natural world**, becoming higher forces that shape reality itself.

Their true histories are fragmented. Over centuries their stories have been distorted into religious traditions, symbolic titles, and mythic interpretations.

Most citizens worship **titles and aspects** of these beings rather than their original identities.

Scholars refer to the divine beings themselves as **The Ascended Pantheon**.

---

# Structure of the Pantheon

The pantheon currently consists of **nine known ascended beings**.

These entities embody major forces that influence the world:

- civilization
- life
- nature
- death
- knowledge
- silence and hidden truth
- war and rulership
- cosmic balance and debt
- darkness and predation

Many temples worship only one or two members of the pantheon, but most cultures acknowledge the existence of all nine.

---

# Aurelion, The First Flame

**Modern Faith:** The Last Ember  
**Domains:** Light, Forge  
**Symbols:** Braziers, anvils, burning crowns

### Appearance

Aurelion appears as a tall bronze-skinned figure whose body resembles cracked metal with fire glowing beneath. His crown is a ring of floating embers.

### Mortal Legend

Aurelion was once a blacksmith during an age of endless winter. When civilization's final city began to lose its last fires, he refused to let the great forge die.

He entered the furnace and held the flame alive until it consumed him.

When the fire settled, he emerged transformed.

### Influence

Aurelion represents:

- fire
- civilization
- craftsmanship
- perseverance

Blacksmiths, builders, and city watchmen commonly invoke his name.

---

# Elyra, The Weeping Mother

**Modern Faith:** The Weeping Mother  
**Domains:** Life  
**Symbols:** Veiled statues, silver tears, open hands

### Appearance

A veiled woman in flowing white cloth whose hidden face continually sheds tears of silver light.

### Mortal Legend

Elyra was a healer during a devastating plague. She refused to abandon the sick even after losing her own family.

She continued treating victims until the disease claimed her.

When she awoke, the plague had vanished from the city.

### Influence

Elyra represents:

- compassion
- healing
- suffering
- birth and care

Healers, midwives, and caretakers commonly follow her teachings.

---

# Kael Thornborn, The Thorn Father

**Modern Faith:** The Thorn Father  
**Domains:** Nature, Tempest  
**Symbols:** Antlers, thorned branches, lightning

### Appearance

A towering figure with antlers and bark-like skin, surrounded by twisting vines and storm clouds.

### Mortal Legend

Kael was once a hunter who rejected the spread of cities into the wilderness.

He vanished into the deep forests for years.

When he returned, the land itself responded to his presence. Forests grew where he walked and storms followed him.

### Influence

Kael represents:

- wilderness
- storms
- natural balance
- the power of untamed lands

Rangers, hunters, and rural communities revere him.

---

# Morvain, The Pale Shepherd

**Modern Faith:** The Pale Shepherd  
**Domains:** Death, Grave  
**Symbols:** Lanterns, shepherd crooks, grave markers

### Appearance

A pale cloaked figure carrying a lantern of ghostly blue flame and a shepherd's staff.

### Mortal Legend

Morvain was a grave keeper during a catastrophic war.

When restless spirits rose from battlefields, he walked among them and called them to follow him.

Thousands of spirits obeyed and vanished into the night behind him.

### Influence

Morvain represents:

- death
- burial
- guidance of souls
- peaceful passage beyond life

Funeral priests and grave wardens honor him.

---

# Valtheris, The Crowned Witness

**Modern Faith:** The Crowned Witness  
**Domains:** Knowledge, Order  
**Symbols:** mirrored crowns, books, quills

### Appearance

A robed figure wearing a crown made of small mirrors, each reflecting a different historical moment.

### Mortal Legend

Valtheris was a judge obsessed with preserving truth.

Before his kingdom fell, he sealed its entire history inside a single book.

When the book was discovered centuries later, the ink appeared freshly written.

Valtheris himself had vanished.

### Influence

Valtheris represents:

- truth
- memory
- law
- historical record

Archivists and scholars revere him.

---

# Seraphine the Silent

**Modern Faith:** The Silent Choir  
**Domains:** Twilight, Knowledge  
**Symbols:** sealed lips, blank scrolls, candles

### Appearance

A calm woman surrounded by floating parchment pages written in an unreadable script.

Her mouth is always closed.

### Mortal Legend

Seraphine believed spoken language corrupted truth.

She lived in silence for decades observing the world.

Eventually she wrote a single book.

Those who read it claimed to hear voices in their minds.

### Influence

Seraphine represents:

- silent knowledge
- contemplation
- hidden truths

Her followers often take vows of silence.

---

# Imperion, The Gilded Throne

**Modern Faith:** The Gilded Throne  
**Domains:** War, Order  
**Symbols:** golden thrones, crossed swords, crowns

### Appearance

An armored king seated upon a floating throne made from fused golden weapons.

### Mortal Legend

Imperion united many warring kingdoms into a single empire.

At the height of his power he declared that rulership itself was divine.

After his death, his throne rose into the sky and remained suspended there.

### Influence

Imperion represents:

- conquest
- leadership
- authority
- military order

Soldiers and rulers often invoke him.

---

# Nyxara, The Debt Unending

**Modern Faith:** The Black Tithe  
**Domains:** Trickery, Death  
**Symbols:** black coins, chains, masked figures

### Appearance

A shadowed figure wrapped in chains made of coin-shaped links, wearing a polished obsidian mask.

### Mortal Legend

Nyxara was a thief who stole from kings and temples alike.

One night she attempted to steal from a god.

She returned changed. Her shadow moved independently and debts began to follow her wherever she went.

Eventually she vanished, but the debts remained.

### Influence

Nyxara represents:

- bargains
- sacrifice
- cosmic balance
- hidden costs

Her worship is usually secretive.

---

# Nerscylla, The Veiled Night

**Modern Faith:** Rare or forbidden  
**Domains:** Darkness, Blood, Undeath  
**Symbols:** blood crescents, spider-like sigils, chalices

### Appearance

A pale woman shrouded in black veils with crimson eyes. From her back extend long shadow-like limbs resembling spider legs.

### Mortal Legend

Nerscylla was a noblewoman obsessed with immortality.

She discovered a forbidden ritual that granted her eternal existence through blood.

Those she fed upon began to rise like her.

Entire bloodlines became creatures of the night.

### Influence

Nerscylla represents:

- vampirism
- predation
- immortality through corruption
- the hidden aristocracy of monsters

Her followers include vampire covens and secret blood cults.

---

# The Pantheon's Legacy

Although the Ascended are widely acknowledged, their **true histories are mostly forgotten**.

Most modern religions worship simplified titles such as:

- The Last Ember
- The Weeping Mother
- The Pale Shepherd

Scholars believe these traditions are **fragmented interpretations of the Ascended themselves**.

Ancient texts hint that the Ascended may have once interacted directly with mortal kingdoms.

Some theories suggest the rise and fall of **The Crown** may be connected to these divine beings.

If true, the relationship between the Ascended and the Crown could explain many mysteries of the world.
