# Oltheros

**Status:** **Lost kingdom** — predates modern **Aaldharyn**  
**Heraldry:** **Hawk**  
**Fate:** Homeland now lies beneath the **Temples of the Ascended** ([mount Embermaw](../mount_embermaw.md))

**Sources:** [Aaldharyn World Lore](../Aaldharyn_World_Lore.md) · Play: [Session 6](../../06_sessions/session_06.md) (maps), [Session 8](../../06_sessions/session_08.md) (named by Demerin scholars)

---

## Snapshot

**Oltheros** was a kingdom or people that existed **before** the unified kingdom of Aaldharyn. House **Demerin** scholars identified symbols on **Crown-era maps** and **pre-Crown Orins** as Oltherosi in origin.

Characteristics associated with Oltheros in play:

- **Hawk heraldry**
- Clothing patterns matching **Devon Ebner's** attire
- Symbols matching recovered **Orins**
- A site near **Soulstone** on maps that **no longer exists** on modern charts

---

## Hidden truth (from play)

Demerin researchers reported that Devon's remembered homeland gathered **immense spiritual power** and may have contributed to the creation of a **divine plane**. The region is now buried under the **Temples of the Ascended**.

**General Sten Rowe** — a title using **General** rather than modern **High Marshal** — surfaced in Devon's memory while studying the maps ([Session 6](../../06_sessions/session_06.md)). The name and title feel **ancient**.

- Oltheros is **Devon's** origin thread — tie to **clockwork soul**, **amnesia**, and **reborn** nature without resolving it early.
- Hawk heraldry may **conflict** or **align** with Greifenhald / military symbolism — use as misdirection or kinship.
- Spiritual power under the Ascended temples links Oltheros to **pantheon ascension** lore if desired.
