# Session 5 — Soulstone (GM scratch)

*Non-canon planning notes. Canon summaries: [`../06_sessions/`](../06_sessions/).*

---

## At a glance

**What this session is**

- Party opens in **Soulstone** (Greifenhald fortress, containment under the city).
- **Sun comes back** after the long red-moon stretch — big emotional beat, not necessarily “we won.”
- **Ashcombe** pressure on Greifenhald turf (roads, charters, observers) — politics tied to **seals**, not just map color.
- **Mystery:** **Erendor** (dead scribe, lycanthrope on paper) — trail shows someone was **cataloguing New Bloods**.
- **Set-piece:** public **Thrall execution**; good moment for **Ansel**’s Aurelion / tabernacle pull.
- **Payoff:** **Seris’s letters** tie Ashcombe ops to **Crown-era / Pale** creep (draft text at bottom).

**Suggested run order**

1. Arrival (gate or sewer mouth)  
2. Sun return (first big outdoor beat)  
3. Ashcombe vs Greifenhald friction  
4. Erendor clues (sprinkle while they move)  
5. Thrall execution  
6. Letter reveal (when you choose)

*Reorder if the table needs it — but try to land the sun in the **first major public** scene.*

## Player-facing logline

You can read this (or paraphrase) at the start:

- You reach **Soulstone** after the sewers and **Sir Caldric’s pardon**.
- The **sun is back** — the sky finally feels like itself again.
- The city should feel like safety. It doesn’t: **Ashcombe** is everywhere diplomacy used to be, the garrison is tense, and the crowd is building for a **Thrall execution**.
- Everyone will be asked, in some way, to choose between **order**, **faith**, and what sleeps **under** the kingdom.

## Scenes — full notes

### Scene 1 — Arrival (gates or tunnel mouth)

| Sense | Idea |
|-------|------|
| Sight | Greifenhald banners; **newer Ashcomb** colors on a charter wagon or pilgrim column |
| Sound | Drills, farriers; less “festival” than Sunstone — **military** rhythm |
| Smell | Oil, lime; optional faint **wrong** sweetness if you want seal undertone later |

**Play**

- Guards may match “warforged / owl / Last Ember priest” to wanted gossip; **pardon** + Caldric or Ravencroft name if pushed.
- Whisper you can drop: *“Sky broke over the eastern ridge last night.”* (foreshadows sun without explaining)

### Scene 2 — Sun return

**When:** First time they’re in real **open air** — wall walk, market, or execution square while it’s still prepping.

**Crowd**

- Weeping, laughing, someone sick with relief.
- A child: *“Is the moon dead?”*

**Power**

- Greifenhald officers watch the light like **intel**, not comfort.
- Ashcombe-flavored line: *“The Ember judges us.”*

**Tone**

- **Ambiguous triumph** — the world looks “normal” in a city built on **abnormal** containment.

---

### Scene 3 — Political pressure (Ashcombe encroachment)

**Show it through people and papers, not only soldiers**

- Chartered **healers**, **Last Ember** missionaries (not necessarily Ansel’s people).
- **Supply bids** for forward forts; a **noble observer** with **new Ashcomb** on a cloak lining.

**Greifenhald angle**

- *“Circle law says who holds this road.”*
- They **distrust Ashcombe’s Crown ties** — one line of tension, no lecture.

**Optional encounter**

- Ashcombe wagon stopped and searched — PCs asked to **witness** (social + future hook).

## Why the sun returns (GM default)

**One line for yourself**

> Thrall killed in Sunstone sewers + Caldric’s **burn** released a pulse of binding energy that was **clogging** the skyward “channel” the red moon sat in. **Symptom shifted**, seals not “fixed.”

**A bit more**

- The moon was never *only* weather.
- Pressure **moved** — Soulstone containment can **twitch** the same hour (tremor, whispers, Exanima wrist flicker).

**At the table**

- PCs need not get one true answer. NPCs argue: Ember miracle, army secret, *“the thing in the tunnel died.”*

**If you change your mind later**

- Swap to Ashcombe ritual-at-range or Greifenhald geomancy — but keep **one** backbone per arc.

## Erendor mystery — step by step

*Goal:* New Bloods feel **exposed** and dangerous without solving the whole campaign.

**A — Rumor** (gate sergeant, tavern)  
Erendor, scribe, died of **“animal attack.”** Body **burned** (contagion law). → *There was an inquest.*

**B — Records** (Greifenhald or Veil clerk)  
File **sealed** “from above.” Cause: **lycanthrope.** Date lines up with Session 4 night. → *Someone fixed the story.*

**C — Lodgings** (warrant, bribe, or Elliot’s name)  
Half-finished **list**: names + notes like *sundown habits*, *silver*, *mirror* — amateur **New Blood** checks. → *He was hunting.*

**D — Scrap**  
Mention of **“three marked in Sunstone”** (ties to Eldros / Nerscylla thread). → *Same conspiracy layer.*

**E — Optional second corpse**  
Same MO as Erendor, **fresh**; killer drops **Ashcombe** token **or** **Ravencroft** seal. → *Politics, not just monsters.*

**F — Session payoff**  
PCs poking Seris gear + Erendor → **invited**, **warned**, or **detained** — your pace.

**Why Soulstone feels jumpy**

- Caldric’s line: New Bloods feed **hysteria** and **new Thralls**. Erendor died where **army** and **seals** meet.

## When to reveal Seris letters

Pick one (you can combine).

1. **Sunlight** — Wax has a Crown-era micro-sigil; it **cracks** in true daylight (pairs well with sun return).  
2. **Inspection** — Gate confiscates “foreign mail”; clerk opens a **copy**; PCs recover or bargain.  
3. **Seris lived** — Dead drop in Soulstone; **page two** references the first letter.  
4. **Jade pendant** — Buzzes near execution or sewer outflow → **cache** where letters were meant to be filed.

**Seris’s allegiance**

- Leave it **muddy**: Ravencroft watch + Ashcomb seal + Lamplight orders can mean cover, triple game, or trophies.

### Sealed letter (new Ashcomb wax)

> **To the needle at Sunstone,**
>
> The old blood argues over **which sleeper** to wake. They mistake **inheritance** for **permission**. Your hunt was **sanctioned**; your **trance** was **not** requested—do not make a **third** noise.
>
> If the **Shield** (Greifenhald) locks the southern roads, **move** the package by **the vein** (sewers, supply train, or literal convoy—your call). The **voices below the gray city** grow **polite** when fed; deny them **nothing** that is already **forfeit**.
>
> When the **wrong coffin** opens, the **right** one listens. You will know the **Pale Court’s** breath by the **stillness** after slaughter—not by the sermons of the Ember.
>
> Burn this if you must; **memory** is not your enemy. **Order** without **ascension** is.
>
> — **V.** *(or unsigned)*

**Decode for yourself**

- “Wrong coffin” → Eldros / Ashcombe squabble (Session 3).  
- “Voices below gray city” → Soulstone containment.  
- “Pale Court” → player-facing nickname for **Pale Ascendant** lineage (no theology essay).

---

### Scrap — *“Your mission from Lamplight”*

> **LAMPLIGHT — eyes only**
>
> Confirm **seal fatigue** at the square. If the **Throne’s hounds** (Ravencroft / Blackthorne) sniff toward **Sunstone**, **redirect** east. **Do not** break cover for **silver** or **wolf** business unless the **line** signals.
>
> **Secondary:** If **new curse-blood** (New Bloods) shows **organization**, **document** and **withhold** from the Veil until we **compare** to the **old bindings**.

**Decode for yourself**

- Square / seal → Session 1 Lamplight.  
- “Withhold from Veil” → pressure on **Elliot**.  
- “Old bindings” → Thralls / Crown.
