# The Scarlet Bramble Clan

*Faction reference — cultural background*

This document describes the **Scarlet Bramble Clan**, the forest community whose members share traditions and governance. It is what people raised in the clan are generally taught to believe and how they are taught to see the world.

Some traditions are old and mysterious. Even the elders do not claim to understand every origin of their customs.

**Geography:** For the physical region (the bramble canopy itself), see [Scarlet Bramble](../01_world/scarlet_bramble.md).

---

## The Scarlet Bramble

The Scarlet Bramble Clan lives within a deep forest not far from the settlement of **Lamplight**.

Their home lies beneath a massive dome of crimson thorned vines known simply as **the Scarlet Bramble**.

The bramble forms a living canopy over the clan’s settlement. Its thick vines twist together high above the ground, allowing sunlight to pass through dark red leaves that tint the entire village in a dim crimson glow.

The vines are strong and nearly impossible for outsiders to cut through. Paths into the settlement are hidden among the forest trails and known only to members of the clan.

To the people of the Scarlet Bramble, the vines are not just plants. They are a **living presence that protects the clan and the forest around them**.

---

## The Crimson Ascension

Generations ago, long before the current generation was born, the druids of the clan performed a powerful ritual known as the **Crimson Ascension**.

Soon after the ritual, the strange red vines began to grow from the forest floor. Over time they spread outward and upward until they formed the dome that now shelters the clan.

Most members of the clan believe the ritual awakened a powerful natural force that protects their people.

Because of this belief, the Scarlet Bramble is treated with reverence.

Members of the clan rarely cut its vines unless absolutely necessary, and many rituals are performed beneath its canopy.

---

## Beliefs

The Scarlet Bramble Clan believes that nature is not gentle or kind.

Nature is **balance**.

The forest contains beauty, growth, predation, decay, storms, and quiet renewal. All of these forces exist together, and none can be removed without harming the whole.

Because of this philosophy, the clan respects:

- predators and prey alike
- storms and calm weather
- life and death as part of the same cycle

Druids of the clan often say:

*"The forest survives because nothing stands above the balance."*

---

## The People of the Clan

The Scarlet Bramble Clan is made up of many different peoples who chose to live in harmony with the forest.

The clan does not define itself by ancestry or bloodline.

Anyone who respects the balance of the wild and proves they can live within it may eventually become part of the clan.

Because of this, the Scarlet Bramble contains a mixture of forest-adapted peoples.

Common members include:

- Humans
- Wood Elves
- Firbolgs
- Owlin
- Tabaxi
- Lizardfolk
- Halflings

To outsiders, the clan can seem unusual. The settlement often feels less like a village and more like a gathering of forest spirits.

---

## Owlin in the Scarlet Bramble

Owlin are uncommon in the wider region but respected within the clan.

Their silent flight and keen night vision make them valuable watchers of the forest.

Owlin often serve as:

- night sentinels
- messengers between forest paths
- observers of weather and migration patterns
- scouts who watch the forest from above

---

## Clan structure

The Scarlet Bramble Clan is guided by a **Tribal Chief** who serves as both a spiritual leader and mediator.

The Chief is advised by an **Elder Circle**, made up of the oldest and most experienced druids.

Important roles:

| Title | Responsibility |
|:-----|:-----|
| **Chief** | The voice and guide of the clan |
| **Elder Circle** | Advisors for important decisions |
| **Thornkeepers** | Druids who study and tend the Scarlet Bramble |
| **Pathfinders** | Scouts who patrol the forest and guide travelers |
| **Beastwardens** | Hunters and protectors who maintain balance between predator and prey |
| **Circle Druids** | Druids who follow specific traditions (e.g. the **Circle of Scales**) |

---

## The Circle of Scales

The **Circle of Scales** is a druidic path followed by some clan members. It studies ancient and primal creatures of the world.

Members believe that scaled creatures represent some of nature’s oldest and most enduring forces.

These druids study:

- serpents
- great reptiles
- draconic creatures
- ancient predators

To them, these beings represent survival, resilience, and the unforgiving truths of the wild.

---

## Relationship with Lamplight

Though the clan prefers isolation, they maintain a cautious relationship with the nearby settlement of **Lamplight**.

Occasional trade occurs.

The clan sometimes provides:

- rare herbs
- medicinal plants
- guidance through forest paths

In return, traders from Lamplight bring:

- metal tools
- cloth
- salt
- lantern oil

Most people in Lamplight see the druids as strange but trustworthy guardians of the forest.

---

## The clan and the balance of nature

Members of the Scarlet Bramble Clan view themselves as **caretakers of the forest rather than its rulers**.

They believe civilization exists only because nature allows it.

This belief shapes how the clan treats outsiders and unusual beings.

---

## How the clan views constructs (warforged)

Constructs such as **warforged** are rare and puzzling to members of the Scarlet Bramble.

Unlike animals, plants, or spirits, constructs do not appear to belong to the natural cycle of life and death.

Because of this, many druids initially view constructs with uncertainty.

**Curiosity** — Some druids wish to understand how a being can move and think without the presence of natural life.

**Caution** — Others worry that constructs may represent forces that exist outside the balance of nature.

**Respect through action** — Members of the clan ultimately judge individuals by their actions. If a warforged demonstrates respect for the forest and its creatures, they are treated as an ally rather than an outsider.

Among the Circle of Scales, constructs are sometimes compared to **ancient stone creatures or living statues** described in old druidic stories: unnatural perhaps, but still capable of existing within the world.
