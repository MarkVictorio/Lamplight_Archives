# Order of the Waning Veil

**Also referenced as:** Order of the Pale Shade (early observation)  
**Type:** Secret order — multi-generational, vampire-aligned leadership  
**Status:** Active antagonist faction (as of Session 17)

**Sources:** Play [Sessions 10–17](../06_sessions/session_10.md) · [Evander Holyfield](../03_npcs/characters/evander_holyfield.md) · [campaign state](../06_sessions/campaign_state_after_session_17.md)

---

## Snapshot

The **Order of the Waning Veil** is a hidden organization whose members are often **born into service**. Its agents pursue **Pale Ascendant** relics and an incomplete **ascension** agenda. A **central vampire** (no public name) outranks most operatives and was angered by the party's vampire kills.

**Eldros Valgrim** is regarded as a **traitor / heathen** who abandoned the Order. The Order's goals clash with Eldros's stated ideal: restore predator/prey balance, not maintain humanity as a controlled servant population.

## Relationship to Eldros

- Eldros confirms **Vaelroth Valgrim is his father** ([session 15](../06_sessions/session_15.md)).
- Eldros aims to **break Vaelroth's seal**, **purge the Waning Veil**, and restore what he calls the **natural order**.
- The Church also suspects Eldros's line ties to the **Last Vampire** ([session 14](../06_sessions/session_14.md)).

---

## At the table

- Use the Order for **infiltrated nobles**, relic heists, and **suicide-protocol** cult behavior.
- Do not reveal the central vampire until earned; Evander's partial knowledge is the model for most agents.
- Contrast Order **ascension** mystery with Eldros's **predator/prey** philosophy for moral gray space.
