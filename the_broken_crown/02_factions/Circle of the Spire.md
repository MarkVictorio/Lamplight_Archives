# The Circle of the Spire

*A Canon Reference Document*

---

## Overview

The Circle of the Spire is the governing body formed after the fall of the Crown. It represents a fragile balance of power between five noble houses, each controlling a fundamental pillar of the kingdom.

This balance is not true equality, but a maintained illusion designed to prevent collapse or domination.

---

## The Five Houses

### House Ravencroft

**Title:** The Voice of Law
**Authority:** Highest decree in court

Ravencroft serves as the final legal authority within the Circle. Their rulings settle disputes between houses and define what is considered lawful within the kingdom.

**Order:** The Blackthorne

* An elite knightly order loyal only to Ravencroft
* Enforcers of court decisions and execution of high decrees
* Known for absolute discipline and unquestioning loyalty

**Public Role:** Judges, arbiters, lawmakers
**Reality:** Their authority exists because it is upheld by the other houses

---

### House Valemyr

**Title:** Keepers of What Remains
**Authority:** Knowledge, archives, and preservation

Valemyr controls the majority of recorded knowledge in the kingdom. They maintain libraries, historical records, and trained scholar-knights.

**Order:** The Argent Vale

* A combined order of scribes and knights
* Protectors of knowledge and enforcers of archival authority
* Operate both as historians and battlefield tacticians

**Public Role:** Scholars, historians, archivists
**Reality:** They determine what history survives

---

### House Greifenhald

**Title:** The Shield of the Kingdom
**Authority:** Military power

Greifenhald commands the largest military force in the kingdom. They maintain fortifications, armies, and city defenses.

**Public Role:** Protectors, generals, defenders
**Reality:** They hold the greatest capacity for open force

---

### House Ashcombe

**Title:** The Blood That Remembers
**Authority:** Lineage tied to the old Crown

Ashcombe maintains its position through ancient lineage. Their estates and influence trace back to the era of the Crown.

**Public Role:** Noble lineage, ceremonial authority
**Reality:** Their connection to the Crown may hold dangerous truths

---

### House Demerin

**Title:** The Quiet Ledger
**Authority:** Historical consistency and mediation

Demerin oversees the validation of records and maintains continuity between conflicting accounts of history. They act as neutral mediators between houses.

**Public Role:** Historians, mediators, record keepers
**Reality:** They preserve the kingdom’s shared version of truth

---

## Heads of the Houses

### House Ravencroft

**Lord Justiciar Alistair Ravencroft**

A cold and deliberate arbiter whose rulings rarely favor mercy. Known for his belief that law must remain unyielding to preserve order.

### House Valemyr

**Lady Archivist Elowen Valemyr**

A composed and enigmatic scholar-knight. Speaks rarely in court, but when she does, her words carry the weight of recorded truth.

### House Greifenhald

**High Marshal Odran Greifenhald**

A war-hardened commander who views peace as a temporary condition. Commands loyalty through strength and presence.

### House Ashcombe

**Lord Regent Lucien Ashcombe**

A soft-spoken noble with an unsettling awareness of ancient customs. Carries himself as though he remembers things others do not.

### House Demerin

**Chronicler Maelis Demerin**

An unassuming figure who rarely takes sides openly. Known for an almost unnatural memory and precise speech.

---

## Structural Balance

Each house represents a core pillar of power:

* Ravencroft → Law
* Valemyr → Knowledge
* Greifenhald → Force
* Ashcombe → Legacy
* Demerin → Continuity

No single house can dominate without destabilizing the entire structure.

---

## Known Tensions

### Law vs Truth

Ravencroft and Valemyr often clash over the interpretation versus the recording of events.

### Strength vs Legitimacy

Greifenhald’s military dominance stands in contrast to Ashcombe’s claim of ancient right.

### Control vs Balance

Valemyr’s control over knowledge conflicts with Demerin’s role in maintaining consistency.

### The Crown Question

All houses maintain unease regarding Ashcombe’s connection to the Crown. The return of Crown-era truths could undermine the legitimacy of the entire Circle.

---

## Notable Historical Events

### The Sealing of the Pale Ascendant

During the early formation of the Circle, the founders of House Ravencroft and House Valemyr were present at the sealing of a powerful vampiric entity known in fragmented records as *the Pale Ascendant*.

This moment marked one of the earliest fractures between the philosophies of the two houses.

* **House Valemyr (Argent Vale)** argued that the entity should be preserved and brought to judgment. They believed understanding its nature was essential, and that even such a being should face recorded justice.

* **House Ravencroft (Blackthorne)** rejected this stance entirely. They pushed for immediate binding and permanent removal, arguing that some evils exist beyond judgment and must be erased from the world.

The final decision favored Ravencroft’s position. The entity was sealed rather than studied or judged.

**Lasting Impact:**

* Established the ideological divide of **justice vs eradication**
* Deepened mistrust between the Blackthorne and the Argent Vale
* Created gaps and contradictions in official records regarding the event
* Marked one of the first known suppressions of knowledge in the Circle’s history

## Notes for Canon Consistency

* The Circle was formed after the fall of the Crown
* Each house rose to fill a specific power vacuum
* Much of Crown-era history remains lost, fragmented, or intentionally obscured
* Control of information is as powerful as control of armies

---

## Tone and Use

This document represents the **publicly understood structure** of the Circle of the Spire.

Hidden truths, secret alliances, and forbidden knowledge should be layered on top of this foundation during play.
