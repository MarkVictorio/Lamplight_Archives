## Background (from play)

Mal Lucius travels as a professional hunter. His firearm ties him to the **Ravenwatch** gunsmith lineage — uncommon in Aaldharyn and a clear marker of both skill and outsider status on the road.

He entered the chronicle during the party's push through **Soulstone**'s sewers and the competing vampire factions hunting **Pale Ascendant** relics.
