## Amnesia and name

Devon has no idea who he is or why he is still around. All he knows is that he woke up one day in the clothes on his back, walking, **without needing to breathe** — useful when hopefuls have tried to drown him into submission.

He vaguely remembers the name **“Devon”** and liked the sound of it. He took **“Ebner”** from a sign in a town he passed through. **Devon Ebner** was reborn into this land.

---

## Body and mask

He knows his body is not quite normal: the stillness of his chest feels wrong, and there is an almost deafening silence where a heartbeat used to be — offset by the **constant ticking** of a clock somewhere inside him.

A **burn mark** sits on the upper right side of his face, just under his mask; he does not know how he got it. He has invented stories when pressed and hopes not to run into the same people again, having forgotten which tale he told.

---

## Visions

He does not truly sleep. When all is still at night, **visions** come: fire, faces he has never seen, laughter and mockery, grandeur and dullness — a tangled web worse than the politics of the era.

---

## Why he travels

He is looking for **something** — he does not know what. Some would say boredom, or memories (he is not especially troubled by their absence), or **answers**. He is curious why he is still here, but mostly content to live another day.
