## Upbringing and outlook

Séaghdha was raised in the **Scarlet Bramble Clan** near **Lamplight**. For full culture, beliefs, clan roles, trade with Lamplight, and how the clan sees constructs, see the [faction reference](../02_factions/Scarlet%20Bramble.md).

Within the clan, Owlin are valued as night watchers, messengers, and high scouts — a role Séaghdha grew up around.

Séaghdha follows the **Circle of Scales**, studying serpents, great reptiles, draconic beasts, and ancient predators as expressions of endurance and harsh natural truth.

---

## Place in the clan

Séaghdha is considered one of the more promising young druids of the Scarlet Bramble.

The clan’s chief believes Séaghdha may one day become a leader of the people.

Leadership of the Scarlet Bramble requires more than spiritual connection to the forest — it requires knowledge of the wider world. Séaghdha was encouraged to leave the forest for a time and travel beyond the clan’s lands.

The hope is that one day Séaghdha will return with greater understanding of the world beyond the trees, and decide what kind of leader they wish to become.
