# Elliot's Background Brief

## Paladin-Scribe of the Argent Veil

This document outlines knowledge Elliot would reasonably possess based
on his training, research, and service within the **Argent Veil**.

---

# The Argent Veil

The **Argent Veil** is a knightly order dedicated to protecting
civilization from supernatural threats.

While most soldiers fight wars between kingdoms, the Argent Veil exists
to confront dangers that ordinary people rarely understand.

Their duties include:

- hunting monsters that threaten settlements
- investigating occult disturbances
- protecting dangerous ruins or cursed relic sites
- assisting towns plagued by unnatural events

Because of this, many people see the Argent Veil as **guardians standing
between civilization and the darkness beyond it**.

Members of the order travel widely and often arrive where rumors of
monsters, curses, or strange happenings begin to spread.

---

# Knights and Scribes

The Argent Veil does not rely only on warriors.

Over centuries the order has developed a tradition of **scholars and
scribes** who record encounters with supernatural threats.

These members preserve knowledge that may help future knights survive
encounters with dangerous creatures.

Their responsibilities include studying:

- monster behavior and weaknesses
- ancient records and historical accounts
- reports of supernatural events
- locations of dangerous ruins or sealed sites

Elliot serves in this dual role.

He is both **a paladin of the ancient tenets** and **a scribe-researcher
of the Argent Veil**.

Because of this, Elliot often approaches threats not only with steel,
but with knowledge.

He is trained to:

- identify creatures based on historical descriptions
- recognize signs of occult activity
- interpret old texts and fragmented records
- preserve written accounts of new discoveries

---

# The Ancient Tenets

Paladins of the Argent Veil follow a philosophy often referred to simply
as **The Ancient Tenets**.

These teachings guide knights who face supernatural threats and the
difficult choices that often accompany them.

### Vigilance

Darkness grows when it is ignored. A knight must remain watchful for
dangers others cannot see.

### Restraint

Power without discipline leads to corruption.

### Protection

Strength exists to defend those who cannot defend themselves.

### Truth

Fear and superstition often hide deeper dangers. A knight must seek the
truth behind the unknown.

These principles shaped Elliot's training as both a knight and a
scholar.

# Ser Caldrick Vane

## The Veiled Champion

Among the knights of the Argent Veil, few names command more respect
than **Ser Caldrick Vane**.

Caldrick has served the order for centuries. Because he is an elf, his
long life has never been seen as unusual.

To many within the order he represents the ideal knight.

---

## Appearance

Ser Caldrick is instantly recognizable.

He wears distinctive **ebon plate armor**, polished dark like obsidian
steel.

His helmet is fully enclosed and rarely removed outside the inner ranks
of the order.

Over his armor he wears a **deep blue shoulder-borne cape trimmed in
gold**, fastened at one shoulder in traditional knightly fashion.

Because of this striking appearance, people often recognize him from a
distance.

Many statues across the kingdom depict him exactly this way.

---

## The Hero of Aaldharyn

Caldrick's most famous deed is the slaying of the **Elder Lycan of
Aaldharyn**.

The creature terrorized the forests surrounding the city for years,
killing travelers and hunters alike.

According to the stories told within the Argent Veil, Caldrick hunted
the beast alone through the northern forests for three nights.

On the third night he confronted it beneath the old trees outside the
city and defeated it in single combat.

The beast was burned and its remains sealed beneath stone so it could
never rise again.

After that victory, Caldrick's name became known throughout the region.

Many consider him one of the greatest knights of the Argent Veil.

---

# Elliot's Training

During Elliot's time as an initiate, Ser Caldrick Vane was one of the
knights responsible for instructing new recruits.

While Caldrick rarely stayed in one place long, he occasionally trained
initiates in the deeper philosophy of the order.

He instructed Elliot in:

- discipline in combat
- understanding when monsters must be destroyed and when they must be
    contained
- the importance of knowledge when facing the unknown

Because Elliot also showed talent as a researcher and scribe, Caldrick
encouraged him to study the records of the Argent Veil.

He believed that **knowledge can be as powerful a weapon as steel**.

---

# Specialized Knowledge

## The Sealing of Vampires

During his studies as a scribe of the Argent Veil, Elliot encountered
several rare texts describing how powerful undead creatures were
sometimes **sealed rather than destroyed**.

These methods were used when certain creatures proved too dangerous or
too difficult to kill outright.

One ritual appears repeatedly in fragmented records.

Scholars of the order refer to it as:

**The Rite of Crimson Silence**

This ritual can force a vampire into a dormant state and bind it within
a sealed coffin or containment vessel.

Once bound, the creature may remain imprisoned for centuries.

The ritual requires several elements, including:

- a sanctified binding circle
- consecrated silver chains
- sacred inscriptions invoking opposing divine authority
- a sealed coffin designed to hold the creature

However, one crucial component remains missing from the records Elliot
studied.

Every text describing the ritual references a **rare material
component** required to complete the binding.

Unfortunately, surviving records describe it only vaguely.

Some texts refer to it as:

- *a relic of living flame*
- *a fragment of divine light hardened into stone*
- *a vessel capable of holding the last ember of life*

Because of this missing component, Elliot understands how the ritual
works in theory but cannot yet perform it.

Discovering the missing element may one day prove vital when facing
creatures that cannot easily be destroyed.

---

# The Work of the Veil

Despite centuries of knowledge, the world still holds many mysteries.

Strange creatures appear where they should not exist.

Forgotten ruins sometimes awaken with new dangers.

Ancient threats thought long gone occasionally return.

Most people never encounter these things.

But the Argent Veil exists because **someone must stand against them
when they do**.

And sometimes the line between monster and legend is thinner than anyone
would like to believe.
