The Broken Crown
*A Gothic Investigative Dungeons & Dragons Campaign Setting*

---

# Campaign Overview

The Broken Crown is a gothic investigative campaign set in a decaying kingdom haunted by the remnants of a forgotten royal power known only as **The Crown**.

Centuries ago, the Crown ruled through hidden rites and ancient servants called **Thralls**. These beings were bound to the Crown through ritual and entombed across the kingdom in sealed coffins, preserved beyond the reach of time.

The Crown eventually fell. Its royal authority disappeared, its rituals were forbidden, and its history was slowly erased.

Most citizens believe the Crown was simply another failed dynasty.

Scholars and occultists suspect something far stranger.

Across the kingdom, **ancient seals are beginning to fail**, and some of the Crown's servants are awakening.

The truth of what the Crown was, why Thralls were created, and who is responsible for their return forms the central mystery of the campaign.

---

# Kingdom & geography

The realm is **Aaldharyn**: a fractured kingdom whose strongest fortresses sit on a hidden Crown-era lattice—**control**, **origin**, **ritual**, **containment**, **memory**, and **narrative stability**—maintained (and now failing) through the Great Holds and the [Circle of the Spire](../02_factions/Circle%20of%20the%20Spire.md). The frontier town of **Lamplight** is one of the first places where that system visibly frays.

# Setting Tone

## Gothic Mystery

The setting emphasizes investigation, atmosphere, and historical discovery rather than constant combat.

Players uncover secrets through:

- investigation
- historical clues
- NPC testimony
- exploration of forgotten locations

Many truths about the world are hidden behind layers of myth, religion, and corrupted records.

## Themes

- forgotten history
- slow awakening of ancient forces
- corruption of power
- secrecy among noble houses
- buried truths beneath civilization
- the tension between faith and forbidden knowledge

# Historical Era: The Crown

## The Crown

The Crown refers to the ruling power that dominated the kingdom centuries ago.

It is unclear whether the Crown was:

- a royal bloodline

Most historical records referencing the Crown were destroyed after its fall.

## Fall of the Crown

The Crown eventually collapsed under unknown circumstances.

Historical records describing the fall are incomplete or intentionally erased.

What is known:

- many Crown structures were destroyed
- Crown rituals were outlawed
- surviving relics were sealed or hidden
- Thralls were placed in coffins and sealed beneath the kingdom

Most modern institutions formed **after the fall of the Crown**, often in response to the chaos that followed.

---

# Thralls

## Definition

Thralls are ancient servants created during the era of the Crown.

They appear to be bound to the Pale Ascendant through ritual means and preserved across centuries in sealed coffins.

Thralls are central to the hidden history of the kingdom.

## Known Properties

- Thralls do not appear to age
- Many are stored in ritual coffins or caskets
- Their awakening often requires ritual interference
- Newly awakened Thralls appear weakened or unstable
- Their original purpose remains unknown

## Historical Role

Fragments of Crown-era writings suggest Thralls served as:

- elite servants of the Pale Ascendant
- guardians of important locations
- enforcers of royal authority

The exact process used to create Thralls has been lost.

Some scholars believe Thralls were not originally human.

## Current Situation

Evidence suggests some Thrall coffins are being located and opened.

It is unclear whether this is happening accidentally or through deliberate effort.

# Religion

Religion in the kingdom is fragmented and decentralized.

Most traditions formed after the fall of the Crown and reflect humanity's attempt to understand the forces that shaped the ancient world.

Many citizens follow several religious traditions simultaneously.

## Major Faiths

### The Last Ember
Domains: Light, Forge

The most widespread organized faith in the kingdom.

Followers believe civilization survives through a single remaining spark of sacred light that must be protected from darkness.

Temples maintain eternal flames symbolizing this ember.

Clergy are known as **Keepers**.

---

### The Weeping Mother
Domain: Life

Represents grief, compassion, and endurance through suffering.

Followers serve as healers, midwives, and caretakers of the dying.

Temples are places where grief is openly acknowledged rather than hidden.

---

### The Thorn Father
Domains: Nature, Tempest

Represents the wilderness and the violent balance of the natural world.

Followers believe civilization survives only because nature allows it.

Shrines are commonly located outdoors in forests or hills.

---

### The Pale Shepherd
Domains: Death, Grave

Represents the guidance of souls after death.

Clergy maintain burial grounds and ensure the dead remain undisturbed.

Many communities rely on Shepherd priests for funerary rites.

---

### The Crowned Witness
Domains: Knowledge, Order

Represents truth, memory, and the preservation of history.

Followers serve as archivists, historians, and judges.

Their temples resemble libraries and record halls.

---

### The Silent Choir
Domains: Knowledge, Twilight

A contemplative order that believes truth is discovered through silence and observation.

Members spend long periods in meditation and written study.

---

### The Gilded Throne
Domains: War, Order

Represents authority, conquest, and the belief that civilization requires strong rulers.

Historically supported by noble houses and military orders.

---

### The Black Tithe
Domains: Trickery, Death

A secretive and forbidden doctrine.

Its followers believe death is a debt that must eventually be repaid.

Most kingdoms outlaw its practices.

# Political Structure

The kingdom is currently governed by a collection of noble houses and regional authorities.

After the fall of the Crown, centralized authority weakened and power fragmented.

## Noble Houses

Several noble families maintain influence across the kingdom.

Some houses possess ancient estates, crypts, or ruins that predate the fall of the Crown.

Many houses deny any connection to Crown-era history.

Scholars suspect otherwise.

---

# Hidden Knowledge

## Lost Records

Much of the Crown's history was intentionally erased.

Surviving records are scattered across:

- monastery libraries
- sealed archives
- noble estate vaults
- forgotten crypt networks

## Occult Research

Certain groups continue to study forbidden Crown-era knowledge.

These groups search for:

- Thrall coffins
- ritual fragments
- Crown relics
- lost royal rites

The motivations of these groups vary from curiosity to ambition.

---

# The Central Mystery

Several questions define the deeper mystery of the setting.

## Unanswered Questions

- What was the Crown truly?
- Why were Thralls created?
- Why were Thralls sealed rather than destroyed?
- Who or what caused the fall of the Crown?
- Why are ancient seals now failing?
- Who is awakening the Thralls?

The answers to these questions may reshape the understanding of the entire kingdom.
