# Kael Thornborn, the Thorn Father

**Type:** Ascended (divine figure)  
**Modern faith:** The Thorn Father — domains Nature, Tempest  
**Symbols:** Antlers, thorned branches, lightning

**Source:** [The Ascended Pantheon](../../01_world/The%20Ascended%20Pantheon.md)

## Appearance (mythic)

Towering; **antlers**, bark-like skin, vines and **storm clouds** about him.

## Mortal legend

Hunter who rejected cities eating the wild; vanished into deep forest for years, returned with **forests growing in his wake** and **storms** at his heels.

## Influence

Wilderness, storms, natural balance, untamed lands. Rangers, hunters, rural communities.

## At the table

Wilderness omens, “civilization by permission of the green,” storms as punctuation; **Kael Thornborn** as the name behind the Thorn Father.
