# Nyxara, the Debt Unending

**Type:** Ascended (divine figure)  
**Modern faith:** The Black Tithe (forbidden) — domains Trickery, Death  
**Symbols:** Black coins, chains, masked figures

**Source:** [The Ascended Pantheon](../../01_world/The%20Ascended%20Pantheon.md)

## Appearance (mythic)

Shadowed figure in **chains of coin-shaped links**; **obsidian mask**; **shadow moves on its own**.

## Mortal legend

Thief who robbed kings and temples; tried to steal from a **god**, returned **changed** — debts **followed her** until she vanished, but **debts remained**.

## Influence

Bargains, sacrifice, cosmic balance, hidden costs. Worship is **secretive**; aligned with [Black Tithe](../../01_world/Religions%20of%20the%20Kingdom.md) rumors.

## From play (session notes)

The **Black Tithe** predates the modern word "vampire" ([Session 16](../../06_sessions/session_16.md)). **Devon** linked the ancient figure **Serah'Kiel, the Crimson Seraph** to that faith. Table evidence suggests **Eldros** and **Vaelroth**'s bloodline points to the **Crimson Seraph**, **not** **Nerscylla** — reconcile with Nyxara / Black Tithe pantheon lore as mythic overlap or misidentification until confirmed.

## At the table

Patron for cults, warlocks, blackmail metaphysics; name **Nyxara** likely unknown to common folk.
