# Valtheris, the Crowned Witness

**Type:** Ascended (divine figure)  
**Modern faith:** The Crowned Witness — domains Knowledge, Order  
**Symbols:** Mirrored crowns, books, quills

**Source:** [The Ascended Pantheon](../../01_world/The%20Ascended%20Pantheon.md)

## Appearance (mythic)

Robed figure; crown of **small mirrors**, each showing a **different historical moment**.

## Mortal legend

Judge obsessed with truth; before his kingdom fell he **sealed its entire history in one book**. Centuries later the book surfaced with **ink still fresh** — **Valtheris** was gone.

## Influence

Truth, memory, law, historical record. Archivists and scholars.

## From play (session notes)

From [Session 16](../../06_sessions/session_16.md):

- Roadside monument (Pale Shepherd symbols, arena-like glyphs): **Devon** recognized a figure as the **Ascendant of Order** / **Crowned Witness**.
- Faith is **no longer widely practiced**; some **Ravencrofts** still honor the Witness in secret.
- Table quote tied to the find: *"Nothing in history truly disappears so long as someone remembers it."*

## At the table

Patron for archivists and trials; mirrors Valemyr/Demerin themes — **what is remembered vs what is allowed to be true**.
