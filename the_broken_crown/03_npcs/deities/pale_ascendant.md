# The Pale Ascendant

**Type:** Entity — vampiric power (sealed)  
**Also referenced as:** figure to whom **Thralls** may be ritually bound ([Overview](../../00_core/Overview.md))

**Sources:** [Overview](../../00_core/Overview.md), [Circle of the Spire](../../02_factions/Circle%20of%20the%20Spire.md)

## Snapshot

A powerful **vampiric entity** named in fragmented records. During the early Circle, founders of **Ravencroft** and **Valemyr** were present at its **sealing**.

## What is known

- **Ravencroft (Blackthorne):** demanded immediate binding and permanent removal — some evils beyond judgment, to be erased from the world.
- **Valemyr (Argent Vale):** wanted preservation and **judgment under record**, arguing understanding was essential.
- Outcome: **sealing** as Ravencroft wished; official history has **gaps and contradictions** around the event.

## Thralls (linked lore)

- Thralls are ancient Crown-era servants, possibly **not originally human**; scholars tie them to ritual binding.
- Overview states Thralls appear bound to the **Pale Ascendant** through ritual. Use at table as deep lore or gradual reveal.

## Identity (from play)

At table, the sealed figure is named **Vaelroth Valgrim, the Pale Ascendant** ([Session 15](../../06_sessions/session_15.md)).

- **Eldros Valgrim** confirmed **Vaelroth is his father**; Eldros seeks to **break Vaelroth's seal** ([Session 15](../../06_sessions/session_15.md)).
- Bloodline at table (post–Session 17): **Serah'Kiel, the Crimson Seraph** → **Vaelroth** → **Eldros** → **Seaghdha** — **not** from **Nerscylla's** line ([Session 16](../../06_sessions/session_16.md)–[17](../../06_sessions/session_17.md)).
- Relics tied to the name: **Shroud**, **Pale White Lantern**, **Veil** (flight, invisibility, bat form; stronger for vampires) ([Sessions 13–15](../../06_sessions/session_13.md)).

Reconcile with scholarly "sealed entity" lore above: public history may omit the **Valgrim** name entirely.

## At the table

- Sealed horror, political football, or both; records may lie.
- Bridges noble politics (Circle founding) with occult Crown plotlines.
