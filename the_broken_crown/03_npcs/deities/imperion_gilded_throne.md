# Imperion, the Gilded Throne

**Type:** Ascended (divine figure)  
**Modern faith:** The Gilded Throne — domains War, Order  
**Symbols:** Golden thrones, crossed swords, crowns

**Source:** [The Ascended Pantheon](../../01_world/The%20Ascended%20Pantheon.md)

## Appearance (mythic)

Armored king on a **floating throne** forged from **fused golden weapons**.

## Mortal legend

United warring kingdoms into one empire; at his height declared **rulership itself divine**. After death his **throne rose** and **remained suspended** in the sky.

## Influence

Conquest, leadership, authority, military order. Soldiers, rulers, noble-aligned chapels.

## At the table

Legitimacy sermons, martial chaplains, visual omen (throne in the sky) if you want myth made visible.
