# Morvain, the Pale Shepherd

**Type:** Ascended (divine figure)  
**Modern faith:** The Pale Shepherd — domains Death, Grave  
**Symbols:** Lanterns, shepherd crooks, grave markers

**Source:** [The Ascended Pantheon](../../01_world/The%20Ascended%20Pantheon.md)

## Appearance (mythic)

Pale cloaked figure; **lantern of ghostly blue flame**, shepherd’s staff.

## Mortal legend

Grave keeper in a catastrophic war; when **restless spirits** rose from battlefields, he walked among them and **called them to follow** — thousands vanished into the night behind him.

## Influence

Death, burial, guidance of souls, peaceful passage. Funeral priests and grave wardens.

## At the table

Rites for the dead, hauntings, “proper rest”; ties to kingdom anxiety that **the dead stay down** ([Religions of the Kingdom](../../01_world/Religions%20of%20the%20Kingdom.md)).
