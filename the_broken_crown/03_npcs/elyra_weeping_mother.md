# Elyra, the Weeping Mother

**Type:** Ascended (divine figure)  
**Modern faith:** The Weeping Mother — domain Life  
**Symbols:** Veiled statues, silver tears, open hands

**Source:** [The Ascended Pantheon](../01_world/The%20Ascended%20Pantheon.md)

## Appearance (mythic)

Veiled woman in white; hidden face sheds **tears of silver light**.

## Mortal legend

Healer during a plague; refused to abandon the sick after losing her own family, worked until the disease took her — when she **awoke**, the plague had left the city.

## Influence

Compassion, healing, suffering, birth and care. Healers, midwives, caretakers follow her thread.

## At the table

Emotional anchor for grief-allowed-openly play; **Elyra** vs folk title “Weeping Mother” as reveal for lore-focused PCs.
