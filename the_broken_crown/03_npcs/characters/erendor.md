# Erendor

**Type:** Mortal — **lycanthrope** (forced transformation); former **Argent Veil** scribe  
**Also known as:** —  
**Languages:** **Elvish** (primary); limited Common when distressed  
**Master:** **Ser Caldrick Vane** — Erendor was his **first apprentice**

**Sources:** [Caldrick Vane](caldrick_vane.md) · [Elliot — Argent Veil background](../../07_player_characters/elliot_argent_veil_background.md) · Play: [Session 4](../../06_sessions/session_04.md) (reported dead), [Session 6](../../06_sessions/session_06.md)–[Session 8](../../06_sessions/session_08.md)

---

## Snapshot

**Erendor** was believed **dead in Soulstone** — official records cited an **animal attack** and a **burned body** (contagion law). The party found him **alive** in a hidden facility beneath **Sunstone**, bound in astral chains within a ritual circle after forced lycanthropic transformation.

He speaks **Elvish**, considers himself **impure**, and initially feared **death** more than living as a monster. **Elliot** intervened when Erendor attempted **suicide** during a second transformation; he was subdued and kept alive.

## What the table has seen

From [Session 6](../../06_sessions/session_06.md):

- Captive in a **wolf-like** form; **grappled** and **downed Ansel** before subdual.
- Claims **chains from the mist** separated him from **Caldrick** during a scouting mission.
- Describes a **bald vampire** as responsible; other captives died or became weaker undead.
- Three **peasant survivors** freed from nearby cages corroborate abductions.

From [Session 7](../../06_sessions/session_07.md):

- Stabilized but **near death**; handcuffed for safety when party reunited with **Plagueis** and **Exanima**.
- **Elliot** spent time with him; Erendor fears meeting **Caldrick** again.
- Achieved a **controlled partial shift** (~30 minutes): mentally aware, bestial features, then rapid strain on revert.

From [Session 8](../../06_sessions/session_08.md):

- **Argent Vale** provided **stabilization bracers** (difficult to craft) to help maintain sanity during transformation.
- Purification of corrupted souls is **possible** at **Argent Vale** only — dangerous, possibly **fatal**, closely guarded knowledge.

From [Sessions 11–16](../../06_sessions/session_11.md):

- Transformation **worsened**; party continued seeking ways to preserve his identity.
- **Loretta** opened a sewer route at cost of her arm ([session 11](../../06_sessions/session_11.md)).
- On the Griffin road: found **hidden hole** in mountainside (party sealed it); traveled with party toward **Ebonshard** ([session 16](../../06_sessions/session_16.md)).

## At the table

- Use Erendor for **Argent Veil** moral weight — the order's hero's first student as a **forced monster**.
- **Controlled transformation** is a **resource with cost**, not a solved problem.
- If Caldrick appears, let Erendor's reaction **force** the table to choose truth vs protection.
