**Type:** Mortal-presenting — **vampire** (Newblood); knight of the Argent Veil (public role)  
**Also known as:** The Veiled Champion  
**Titles in play:** Hero of Aaldharyn; mentor / master to **Elliot** (scribe-knight of the Argent Veil)

**Sources:** [Elliot — Argent Veil background (player knowledge)](../../07_player_characters/elliot_argent_veil_background.md), [Nerscylla, the Veiled Night](../deities/nerscylla_veiled_night.md), [The Pale Ascendant](../deities/pale_ascendant.md), [Overview](../../00_core/Overview.md)

## Public legend (what the world believes)

Among the knights of the **Argent Veil**, few names command more respect than **Ser Caldrick Vane**. He has served the order for **centuries**; because he is believed to be an **elf**, his longevity raises no suspicion. To many within the order he represents the ideal knight.

**Appearance (famous):** Instantly recognizable — **ebon plate armor** polished dark like obsidian steel; a **fully enclosed helmet** rarely removed outside the inner ranks; over the armor, a **deep blue shoulder-borne cape trimmed in gold**, fastened at one shoulder. Statues across the kingdom often depict him exactly this way.

**The Hero of Aaldharyn:** His most famous deed is the slaying of the **Elder Lycan of Aaldharyn**, which terrorized the forests for years. Order stories say he hunted the beast alone through the northern forests for **three nights**, confronted it beneath old trees outside the city, and defeated it in single combat. The remains were **burned and sealed beneath stone**.

**Elliot:** During Elliot’s time as an initiate, Caldrick occasionally trained recruits in the deeper philosophy of the order — discipline, when to destroy vs contain monsters, and the value of knowledge. He encouraged Elliot’s work as scribe-researcher, holding that **knowledge can be as powerful a weapon as steel**.

## Core identity (GM)

- **Name:** Caldrick Vane  
- **Nature:** Vampire (**Newblood**)  
- **Status:** Active, influential, and dangerous  
- **Role:** Central figure in the **resurgence of vampirism**

Caldrick is not merely a vampire; he is a key figure in the return of forces long believed eradicated.

---

## The Newblood curse

Caldrick is directly tied to the emergence of **Newbloods**.

### Nature of Newbloods

- Not natural vampires in the old sense  
- Manifestations of **divine conflict**  
- Created as a response to imbalance among higher powers  

### Divine context

- An affront to **Vaelroth, the Pale Ascendant** ([Pale Ascendant](../deities/pale_ascendant.md))  
- A reaction from **Nerscylla, the Veiled Night** ([Nerscylla](../deities/nerscylla_veiled_night.md))  

Nerscylla effectively **“blessed”** new vampires into existence in defiance of Vaelroth’s attempt to replace or supplant her (interpret at table as mythic politics).

### Implications

- Vampirism is no longer only a curse or bloodline accident — it is also **deliberate divine intervention**  
- Caldrick exists inside a **cosmic struggle**, not merely a personal one

## Personal tragedy

Caldrick does **not** treat his condition as a gift.

### Key truth (GM anchor)

His transformation is tied to the **life of his wife**.

### Possible interpretations (choose or leave open)

- He traded her life for power  
- Her death was the **price** of his transformation  
- The transformation was **forced** on him at her expense  

### Emotional core

- Guilt, regret, obsession, internal conflict  

He embodies **power gained through irreversible loss**.

---

## Relationship to Vaelroth

Vaelroth ([Pale Ascendant](../deities/pale_ascendant.md)) was once believed to be the **last vampire** — or the last great vampiric power the Crown truly feared.

### Historical context

- The **Crown** spent immense resources **eradicating** vampires ([Overview](../../00_core/Overview.md))  
- Vaelroth remained the final known entity of that era in many accounts  

### Caldrick’s role

- Represents a **new era** of vampirism  
- Challenges the idea of Vaelroth’s **uniqueness**  
- May read as **rival**, **disruption**, or **consequence** of divine interference  

---

## Connection to Nerscylla

[Nerscylla](../deities/nerscylla_veiled_night.md) is tied to the origin myth of vampirism and forbidden ascension.

### Relationship

- Caldrick may be a **chosen instrument** or an **unwilling recipient** of her power  

### Nuance

- He does **not** necessarily worship her  
- His existence may **conflict** with her intent  

Tension between **divine purpose** and **human grief**.

## Master of Elliot

Caldrick is **master** (patron / mentor) to **Elliot**, a scribe-knight of the Argent Veil.

**Erendor** was Caldrick's **first apprentice** — found alive beneath Sunstone as a **forced lycanthrope** ([Erendor](erendor.md); [Session 6](../../06_sessions/session_06.md) onward). Elliot has been caring for him; reunion with Caldrick is an open wound.

### Narrative role

- Mentor, patron, possible **manipulator**  

### Possible dynamics

- Tragic teacher  
- Calculated guide  
- Architect of a successor  

This ties Caldrick directly to **player progression** and to everything Elliot thinks he knows about honor and the Veil.

## Summary

Caldrick Vane is a **tragic architect** of a new vampiric age — born from **divine defiance** and **personal loss**. Bound to powers he neither fully accepts nor understands, he walks the line between **cursed man** and **chosen instrument**, shaping others even as he struggles with what he has become.
