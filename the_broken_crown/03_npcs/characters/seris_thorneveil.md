## Snapshot

**Seris** presents as **poised** and **hard to read** — a professional who treats Sunstone like a **hunting ground** with rules only she seems to know. She claims responsibility for putting **the whole town in a trance** while she stayed **awake**, and she does not know **why** she was immune. Whether that is **true magic**, **training**, **bloodline**, or **bluff** is unresolved.

She positions herself as trying to **prevent further deaths** in Sunstone and takes a **personal** interest in the **white-haired New Blood** who dragged bodies through the streets.
