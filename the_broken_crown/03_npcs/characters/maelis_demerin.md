# Maelis Demerin

**Type:** Mortal — noble, head of house  
**Title:** Chronicler, House Demerin (The Quiet Ledger)

**Source:** [Circle of the Spire](../../02_factions/Circle%20of%20the%20Spire.md)

## Snapshot

Unassuming; rarely takes sides openly. Known for **almost unnatural memory** and precise speech.

## Authority

- Validates records and keeps **continuity** between conflicting historical accounts; neutral mediator between houses in disputes over “what happened.”
- Public role: historians, mediators, record keepers — the kingdom’s shared version of truth.

## From canon

- **Control vs balance:** Valemyr’s hoard of knowledge vs Demerin’s role in which story officially coheres.

## At the table

- Ideal NPC to deliver **contradictions** in the record without picking a house loudly.
- Can surface a detail everyone else “forgot” — or agreed to forget.
