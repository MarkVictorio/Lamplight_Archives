# Nymera

**Type:** Vampire — **Thornveil** / **Scarlet Bramble** connections  
**Status:** Active in vampire faction conflict (as of Session 11)

**Sources:** Play [Sessions 10–11](../06_sessions/session_10.md)

---

## Snapshot

**Nymera** attacked **Ansel** in the **Soul Cairn**, declaring *"This is for my sister!"* — suggesting a personal vendetta tied to the party or their allies. She fed from **Seaghdha** and stole the **Crown of Scarlet Bramble**, making the artifact contested among vampire factions.

Her belongings linked her to multiple Great Houses: **Celestial** correspondence, an **Ashcombe signet**, and a **Greifenhald ring** on a severed finger.
