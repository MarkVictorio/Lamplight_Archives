# Seraph Ashcombe

**Type:** Mortal-presenting — **unverified** noble identity  
**Claimed house:** **House Ashcombe**  
**Appearance (reported):** Red **Tiefling**; black hair; red eyes

**Sources:** [Lucien Ashcombe](lucien_ashcombe.md) · [Maelis Demerin](maelis_demerin.md) · Play: [Session 7](../../06_sessions/session_07.md)

---

## Snapshot

At the **Soulstone noble ball**, **Bram Blackthorne** flagged a woman introducing herself as **Seraph Ashcombe**. She presents as Ashcombe nobility, but **no records** support the claim.

**Chronicler Maelis Demerin** found **no evidence** connecting her to House Ashcombe. **Bram** does not recognize her. Her true identity remains **unknown**.

---

## What the table has seen

From [Session 7](../../06_sessions/session_07.md):

- Appeared among nobility during the grand gathering after the sun returned.
- Party and allies were spreading warnings about **vampiric infiltration** of the elite.
- No combat or direct dialogue with the party is recorded in session notes — she is a **presence and warning**, not yet a confirmed antagonist.

**Related loot (Session 8):** Seaghdha recovered a **gold necklace** with **Ashcombe markings** and an **amethyst** centerpiece from a female vampire who **escaped** the ballroom fight. Connection to Seraph is **unconfirmed**.
