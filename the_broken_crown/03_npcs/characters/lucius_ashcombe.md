# Lucius Ashcombe

**Type:** Mortal — Ashcombe noble (young)  
**House:** **House Ashcombe**

**Sources:** [Seraph Ashcombe](seraph_ashcombe.md) · [Lucien Ashcombe](lucien_ashcombe.md) (Lord Regent) · Play [Session 17](../06_sessions/session_17.md)

---

## Snapshot

**Lucius Ashcombe** was captured by **Exanima** during the **Griffin** crisis on the road toward **Ebonshard**. He had been on an Ashcombe **coming-of-age hunt** (brown bear) when the Griffin attacked his group.

Lucius provided the party direct intelligence on **Seraph Ashcombe**, **Lady Ashcombe**, and **Lord Regent Ashcombe**. **Seaghdha** later released him after severing a hand as a warning; **Devon** offered to dispel a ring that would otherwise report Lucius's death to the family.

---

## What Lucius reported (Session 17)

- **Seraph** was a **foundling**, not blood Ashcombe; solitary child; close to servants; wrote strange things; collected **bird bones** (unexplained).
- Nobles called Seraph an **abomination**; Lucius does not know why she represented the house at the noble ball.
- **Lord Regent** has changed; reopened **silver mines**; sold **armaments**; strongest lineage through **mother's side**.
- **Lady Elira** missing since the ball (~two weeks).

---

## At the table

- Lucius is a **living witness**, not a reliable moral anchor — fear and house loyalty may color his story.
- Hand loss and death-ring mechanics are ongoing pressure on Ashcombe politics.
