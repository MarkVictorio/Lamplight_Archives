# Record Keeper Jorenfeld

**Type:** Mortal — **Fire Genasi**; House **Demerin** researcher  
**Also known as:** **Joren** (informal; *"just ask for Joren"* at Achroma)  
**Specialty:** **Iron Guardian** research; construct / Exanima-adjacent study

**Sources:** [House Demerin — Mistforge / Achroma](../../01_world/circle_of_the_spire/demerin/README.md) · [Exanima](../../07_player_characters/exanima.md) · Play: [Session 7](../../06_sessions/session_07.md), [Session 8](../../06_sessions/session_08.md)

---

## Snapshot

A **Fire Genasi** bearing **House Demerin** insignia who identified **Exanima** at the Soulstone noble gathering. He reads Exanima as **"defective"** — creating **unintended memories** and behaving beyond designed parameters.

He is scholarly, politically connected within Demerin, and interested in bringing Exanima to **Achroma** for study.
