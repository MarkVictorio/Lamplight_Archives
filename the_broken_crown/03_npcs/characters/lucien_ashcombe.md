# Lucien Ashcombe

**Type:** Mortal — noble, head of house  
**Title:** Lord Regent, House Ashcombe (The Blood That Remembers)

**Source:** [Circle of the Spire](../../02_factions/Circle%20of%20the%20Spire.md)

## Snapshot

Soft-spoken noble with unsettling familiarity with **ancient customs**. Carries himself as if he remembers what others do not.

## Authority

- House position rests on **lineage tied to the old Crown** — estates and influence trace to the Crown era.
- Public role: noble lineage, ceremonial authority.
- Under the surface: connection to the Crown may hold **dangerous truths**; other houses are uneasy about Crown-era revelations.

## From canon

- Central to **strength vs legitimacy** (Greifenhald) and the **Crown question** that could undermine the whole Circle.

## At the table

- Patron, rival, or key witness for Crown-era mysteries; knowledge may exceed what he admits.
- Tone: quiet, old manners, hints of buried ritual or protocol.
