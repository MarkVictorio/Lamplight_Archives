# Evander Holyfield

**Type:** Mortal — Order operative (deceased at table)  
**Affiliation:** **Order of the Waning Veil** (lifelong)  
**Personal tie:** **Leonora** (insisted she was not involved)

**Sources:** [Order of the Waning Veil](../../02_factions/Order%20of%20the%20Waning%20Veil.md) · Play [Sessions 9–15](../06_sessions/session_09.md)

---

## Snapshot

**Evander** first appeared alongside **Leonora** during the **Festival of the Dead** / **Lantern Road** period. The party later observed him exchanging a package and money with **Order of the Waning Veil** members ([session 10](../06_sessions/session_10.md)).

In [session 15](../06_sessions/session_15.md), **Seaghdha** spotted Evander hiding near the party. Restrained and interrogated, Evander revealed he was **born into the Order** and tasked to obtain the **Pale Ascendant Veil**. He named **Vaelroth Valgrim, the Pale Ascendant** and described Eldros as a deserter. The party found **715 crowns** and a protocol note to **end himself** if captured. Evander was killed before he could fully explain the Order's ascension plan; a final letter for **Leonora** was recovered.
