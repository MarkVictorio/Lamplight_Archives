# Seraphine the Silent

**Type:** Ascended (divine figure)  
**Modern faith:** The Silent Choir — domains Twilight, Knowledge  
**Symbols:** Sealed lips, blank scrolls, candles

**Source:** [The Ascended Pantheon](../01_world/The%20Ascended%20Pantheon.md)

## Appearance (mythic)

Calm woman; **floating parchment** in unreadable script; **mouth always closed**.

## Mortal legend

Believed **speech corrupts truth**; lived in silence for decades, observing. Wrote **one book** — readers claimed to **hear voices in their minds**.

## Influence

Silent knowledge, contemplation, hidden truths; followers often **vow silence**.

## At the table

Monastery or archive NPCs who communicate in writing; horror-tinged “true name” of the Choir behind the order.
