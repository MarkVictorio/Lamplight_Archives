# Odran Greifenhald

**Type:** Mortal — noble, head of house  
**Title:** High Marshal, House Greifenhald (The Shield of the Kingdom)

**Source:** [Circle of the Spire](../02_factions/Circle%20of%20the%20Spire.md)

## Snapshot

War-hardened commander who treats peace as temporary. Earns loyalty through strength and presence.

## Authority

- Largest **military force** in the kingdom: fortifications, armies, city defenses.
- Public face: protectors, generals, defenders.

## From canon

- **Strength vs legitimacy** tension with House Ashcombe’s claim of ancient right.
- Holds the greatest **open** capacity for force within the Circle’s balance.

## At the table

- The credible threat of steel when politics stall.
- May be straightforward ally or obstacle depending on whether the party’s goals look like stability or chaos.
