# Elowen Valemyr

**Type:** Mortal — noble, head of house  
**Title:** Lady Archivist, House Valemyr (Keepers of What Remains)

**Source:** [Circle of the Spire](../02_factions/Circle%20of%20the%20Spire.md)

## Snapshot

Composed, enigmatic scholar-knight. Speaks rarely in court; when she does, her words carry the weight of recorded truth.

## Authority

- House controls most **recorded knowledge**: libraries, archives, scholar-knights.
- House order **the Argent Vale** combines scribes and knights — protectors of knowledge and archival authority, historians and battlefield tacticians alike.

## From canon

- Ideological line (historically): argued the Pale Ascendant should be **preserved and brought to judgment**, with nature understood and recorded — opposed Ravencroft’s push for immediate binding and erasure.
- Ongoing tensions: **law vs truth** with Ravencroft; **control vs balance** with Demerin over consistency of the record.

## At the table

- Who decides what history survives; gatekeeper of inconvenient facts.
- Scenes: archives, sealed stacks, quiet testimony that rewrites a room’s understanding of an event.
