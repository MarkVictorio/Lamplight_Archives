# Aurelion, the First Flame

**Type:** Ascended (divine figure)  
**Modern faith:** The Last Ember — domains Light, Forge  
**Symbols:** Braziers, anvils, burning crowns

**Source:** [The Ascended Pantheon](../01_world/The%20Ascended%20Pantheon.md)

## Appearance (mythic)

Tall bronze-skinned figure like **cracked metal with fire beneath**; crown of **floating embers**.

## Mortal legend

Blacksmith in an age of endless winter; when the last city’s fires failed, he **entered the great forge** and held the flame until it consumed him — then **emerged transformed**.

## Influence

Fire, civilization, craftsmanship, perseverance. Invoked by blacksmiths, builders, city watch.

## At the table

Patron flavor for Ember **Keepers**, crafters, and “hold the line” themes; true name **Aurelion** may be scholar-tier knowledge behind the popular title.
