## 3. Blood trail and Seris

Along the path toward the Church: a **blood trail**, **unconscious people** sleeping in the streets, and a sense of **being watched**.

**Seris** (arbalest; poised, unreadable)

- Says she must **prevent more deaths** in town.
- Knows the **white-haired** New Blood; saw her **dragging a body** with purpose — target unclear.
- Claims she put **everyone in Sunstone in a trance**; she alone stayed awake — **she doesn’t know why**.

**Friction**

- Fight breaks out; **Seagh** is **downed**. **Exanima** talks her down. She offers a **potion** to revive Seagh.
- Truce: **non-aggressive** for now.

**Who she is**

- Name (table spelling): **Seris Thorneveil** — [GM sheet](../../03_npcs/characters/seris_thorneveil.md).
- **Lycanthrope hunter**; wolves have grown **stronger** lately.
- **Scout report:** a man appeared in an **abandoned house** with a **casket**; crashes, possible **explosion** / fight.
- She’s needed back in town proper; asks the party to return to the Church by **3 a.m.**
- Her **pocketwatch** bears a **Ravencroft crest** — **Ansel** finds it familiar.

## 5. Cube and safehouse

**Cube**

- Seagh gives the **crimson cube** to Exanima.
- Sync + key → opens into a **metal rectangle** clamped to **Exanima’s left wrist**. It acknowledges his **unit number**; otherwise **dormant**.

**With Seris — the house**

- Battle site **cleaned**; bodies removed for **medical** care.
- **One-story stone house**: dark windows, **bars**, entry via **roof**.
- Interior: **battle damage**, **ice** on walls, **chimney** cold.
- **Eldros’s shirt**, folded — **Thrall blood**; maybe a message.
- **Chest:** more clothes; beneath, a **dagger** — **House Ashcomb**, **older crest** (interests Seris).
- **Exanima** finds **20 crowns**, **Crown-era mint**.
- **Floor:** discolored **pressure plate** → distant **mechanism**. Heavy floorboard — **hidden stairs**.

**Downstairs**

- **Same coffin** as the church — **open**, **stripped**, **empty**.
- Circle drawn in **blood**.
- Seagh **tastes** the blood → visions of **blood and fangs**; a name: **Seraphine** — **mistress of the Last Vampire**.

**The draped corpse**

- Falls from drapes: **upper-class** clothes, **spawn**-like, **heart torn out**, **desiccated**.
- Placed in the casket → ritual residue forms a **small red gemstone** (first time scholars at the table have seen this). The corpse **seems to look** at Seagh.
