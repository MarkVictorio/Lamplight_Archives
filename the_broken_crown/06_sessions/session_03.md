## 2. Church — Eldros’s bargain

When the rest of the party arrives, **Devon** and **Eldros** sit on a pew. Eldros claims he did not want the **other Thrall** awakened either — a **“petty Ashcomb squabble.”** The Ashcombs, he says, are **awakening the wrong one**; a **group** desecrated the church.

**The coffin**

- Belongs to a **Mistress of Val’wrath**.
- Waking her would **hasten the seal’s failure** faster than expected.

**What Eldros wants**

- The **seal** to **hold**, so he remains **free of the Last Vampire’s** control.
- He offers a bargain: *pretend this never happened.* He says **he stopped the ritual**.

**Lore he shares**

- If the **coffin stays sealed**, the church can become whatever the party wants.
- **Slaying** the mistress still **worsens** the seal.
- **Awakening the Lieutenants** of the Last Vampire **breaks** the seal (general knowledge).
- **Three New Bloods**, cursed by **Nerscylla**.
- The party may already hold fragments of **King’s Mercy**.

**What happens**

- Eldros touches the casket; **fog**; he **disappears with the coffin**.
- Only the **summoning circle** remains — **stone**, can be broken.

**Trigger**

- **Exanima** steps on the circle → **red light** underfoot.
- **Ashcomb guards** insist the circle stay untouched → **combat**. The party wins.

## 4. Blackmoor Mausoleum

**Graves**

- A **dug-up, unmarked** grave — **empty**, recent.
- A marked stone: **Cassian Voss** and **Nerella Voss** (the “neighbor” from the rumor).

**Entry**

- A **loud scream** when Seagh peers inside.
- The door won’t budge — **blocked** inside. Another entrance: **cast-iron latch**; traps include **twin spears** (Exanima evades).

**Inside**

- **Sigmar** — bloodied, gagged, scratched, in a chair.
- A woman steps out: the mausoleum is **her house**. She didn’t know Sigmar had friends. Sigmar is a **“subject of her plaything.”** She knows Eldros wants freedom and **fears the New Bloods**. She names herself **a New Blood**.

**Fight**

- She **opens three tombs**; **three figures** attack.
- Combat. She says she’d **rather they ran** — she prefers a **chase**.
- The party presses; she **abducts Sigmar**.
- **Aftermath:** ~**thirty seconds of destruction** (ties to **mausoleum fire** blame in Session 4).
