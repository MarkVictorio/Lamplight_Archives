# Session 10 — The Pale Ascendant's Shadow

---

## At a glance

  ------------ -------------------------------
  **Played**   Notes recorded 2026-06 period
  **Where**    Soulstone, Soul Cairn
  ------------ -------------------------------

The party uncovers deeper connections between the Crown-era world, the
vampires, and the Ascended. Exanima receives further warnings, the Order
of the Waning Veil begins to emerge as a major faction, and Nymera takes
the Scarlet Bramble Crown.

---

## 1. Exanima's Blood Vial

Exanima discovers:

-   A vial used for drawing blood.
-   A letter associated with the Celestial tradition.

The objects suggest that blood and ancient ritual practices remain
connected to the current vampire investigations.

---

## 2. The Dwarven Mystic

A Dwarven Mystic provides information regarding the **Crown of Thorns**.

The party learns that attempts were made to replicate the ritual
associated with the **Scarlet Bramble Crown**.

Two surviving descendants or kin of the original ritualists are believed
to remain:

-   one near Lion's Mane
-   one Tiefling traveling toward Achroma

---

## 3. Evander's Exchange

Evander is observed exchanging a package and money with members of the
**Order of the Pale Shade**, later identified as the **Order of the
Waning Veil**.

This establishes a direct connection between Evander and the
organization.

---

## 4. Ravencroft's Spies

Spies connected to Ravencroft identify Seaghdha.

The information is reported to **Spymaster Velor**.

Seaghdha's identity is now known to forces operating within the Central
Spire.

---

## 5. The Soul Cairn

The party explores the Soul Cairn.

Pale Shepherd priests recognize Exanima as an **Iron Guardian**.

The location contains further evidence that the current religious
institutions are built upon much older structures.

---

## 6. Failsafe

The party encounters **Failsafe**, an ancient Iron Guardian associated
with the **Third Fleet**.

His presence reinforces the idea that the Iron Guardians existed as
organized forces during the older world.

---

## 7. Nymera

A vampire named **Nymera** appears.

She is associated with the Thornveil and has connections to the Scarlet
Bramble.

She attacks Ansel.

### The Message

Nymera tells Ansel:

> "This is for my sister!"

The statement suggests a personal history between Nymera and the people
surrounding the party.

---

## 8. The Scarlet Bramble Crown

Nymera feeds from Seaghdha and takes the **Crown of Scarlet Bramble**.

The theft makes the Crown a contested artifact among the vampires.

---

## 9. The Pale Ascendant

The High Marshal recruits the party to protect a relic associated with
the **Pale Ascendant**.

The party learns that:

-   Soulstone was constructed as an anti-vampire fortress.
-   The fortress dates back to the Old Crown.
-   The Pale Ascendant was important enough to warrant dedicated
    containment.

---

## 10. The Veiled Night

Nymera possesses several significant objects:

-   a Celestial letter
-   an Ashcombe signet
-   a Greifenhald ring attached to a severed finger

The objects suggest connections between vampires and multiple Great
Houses.

## Key Revelations

-   The Scarlet Bramble ritual has been deliberately replicated.
-   Evander is connected to the Order of the Waning Veil.
-   Ravencroft is actively monitoring Seaghdha.
-   Exanima is recognized as an Iron Guardian.
-   Nymera is connected to the Thornveil and Scarlet Bramble.
-   Vampires possess objects belonging to several Great Houses.
-   Soulstone was originally built as an anti-vampire fortress.
