# Session 17 — The Ashcombe Heir

[← Session 16](session_16.md) · [Session ledger](session_ledger.md) · [Session 18 →](session_18.md)

---

## At a glance

  ------------ ----------------------------------------
  **Played**   Notes recorded 2026-09 period
  **Where**    Road near Ebonshard, Griffin territory
  ------------ ----------------------------------------

The party captures Lucius Ashcombe and begins uncovering the extent of
House Ashcombe's connection to the vampire crisis. A traveler from the
Iron Mire Valley joins the investigation, Griffin evidence points toward
provocation by the Demerins, and Sigmar is finally restored from his
malformed Thrall state.

---

## 1. Exanima Returns

Exanima returns to the party.

He brings a living captive.

The captive is identified as:

**Lucius Ashcombe.**

---

## 2. Lucius Ashcombe

Lucius explains that he had been hunting a brown bear as part of an
Ashcombe coming-of-age tradition.

His group was attacked by the Griffin.

He provides information about House Ashcombe and **Seraph Ashcombe**.

---

## 3. Seraph Ashcombe

Lucius explains that Seraph was a foundling.

She was not a blood descendant of the Ashcombe family.

As a child:

-   she kept to herself
-   she was close to maids and servants
-   she wrote strange things
-   she kept bird bones
-   she showed an interest in magic

The bird bones remain unexplained.

No established bird-related lineage or connection is known.

---

## 4. The Ashcombe Family

Lucius reveals that:

-   Lord Regent Ashcombe has changed significantly.
-   Lady Ashcombe was fond of Seraph.
-   Some nobles considered Seraph an abomination.
-   Lucius does not know why Seraph represented House Ashcombe at the
    noble gathering.
-   Lord Regent Ashcombe reopened silver mines.
-   He sold armaments to other Houses.
-   His strongest lineage connection comes through his mother's side.

---

## 5. Lord Regent Ashcombe

The party learns that Lord Regent Ashcombe's behavior is not simply
political.

He has been:

**turned into a Thrall under orders from the Order of the Waning Veil.**

House Ashcombe therefore has a direct connection to the Order's
activities.

---

## 6. Lady Elira

**Lady Elira** has been missing since the noble ball.

She has been missing for approximately two weeks.

Her disappearance remains unresolved.

---

## 7. Lucius Released

Seaghdha allows Lucius to leave.

As a warning, he cuts off Lucius's hand.

Devon offers to dispel the ring that would otherwise report Lucius's
death to his family.

---

## 8. Navanhi

The party encounters **Navanhi**, a traveler from the **Iron Mire
Valley**.

He carries an axe and has been tasked by his tribal elder with
investigating and killing the Griffin.

Completing the task would make him the next Chief.

---

## 9. Galamar

Navanhi seeks a legendary weapon crafted by **Galamar**, a Forge-God
warhammer.

Seaghdha recognizes Galamar as a half-orc and remembers him as the:

**Hidden Ancient of the Seven Swords.**

The connection suggests that Navanhi's quest may intersect with much
older history.

---

## 10. The Griffin Investigation

The party examines the Griffin's attacks.

Evidence suggests:

**The Demerins may have struck first and provoked the Griffin.**

The mountain contains a large crater, but it is not an active volcano.

The party questions why the expected tremors associated with Thrall
awakenings have not disturbed the region.

---

## 11. Vampire Victims

The party discovers bodies bearing:

-   vampire bite marks
-   signs of exsanguination
-   Greifenhald equipment

The victims appear to have been attacked recently.

The evidence confirms that vampires remain active along the road.

---

## 12. Lothar

Lothar is found bleeding near the camp.

Seaghdha heals him.

Lothar explains that the carriage passengers had been instructed to
continue without him if he were captured.

---

## 13. Sigmar

Lothar reveals that **Sigmar** attacked him.

Sigmar has become severely malformed.

The condition resembles the fate of a Thrall who remains without a
master for too long.

The party realizes that Sigmar's condition has progressed too far for
ordinary intervention.

---

## 14. Eldros's Warning

Eldros has previously warned the party that there may be no other way to
save Sigmar once the malformation progresses too far.

The party begins a ritual to restore him.

---

## 15. The Newborn Vampire

The ritual succeeds.

Sigmar rises again.

He is no longer the malformed Thrall he had become.

He emerges as a **newborn vampire**.

The transformation confirms that a Thrall can potentially survive the
loss of a master through a different vampiric state.

---

## 16. The Marshal

A Marshal approaches the party.

He questions whether they are colluding with undead.

He attempts to provoke Devon by exposing blood and suspecting that Devon
is a vampire.

The confrontation reinforces the growing distrust between the party and
the authorities.

## Key Revelations

-   Seraph Ashcombe was a foundling rather than an Ashcombe blood
    descendant.
-   Seraph's childhood bird-bone collection remains unexplained.
-   Lord Regent Ashcombe is a Thrall created under Waning Veil orders.
-   Lady Elira has been missing since the noble ball.
-   Navanhi is investigating the Griffin to earn his position as Chief.
-   Galamar is connected to the ancient Seven Swords.
-   Evidence suggests the Demerins provoked the Griffin.
-   Vampire attacks are occurring along the road.
-   Sigmar was severely malformed after being left without a master.
-   Sigmar is successfully restored as a newborn vampire.
-   Authorities are increasingly suspicious of the party's connection to
    undead.
