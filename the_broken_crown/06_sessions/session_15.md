# Session 15 — The Waning Veil

[← Session 14](session_14.md) · [Session ledger](session_ledger.md) · [Session 16 →](session_16.md)

---

## At a glance

  ------------ ---------------------------------
  **Played**   12--13 September 2026 (notes)
  **Where**    Soulstone, Misthaven, road west
  ------------ ---------------------------------

The party investigates Evander and finally learns the name and ambitions
of the organization behind him. Eldros claims the Pale Ascendant Veil,
reveals his lineage, and explains his desire to restore the natural
order. The party then escapes Soulstone and begins the journey toward
the next region.

---

## 1. The Enchanted Veil

The party identifies the Veil as an enchanted object.

Its effects include:

-   flight
-   invisibility
-   transformation into a bat
-   stronger effects when used by vampires

The item appears relatively simple despite its importance.

---

## 2. Evander

The party discovers **Evander Holyfield** hiding nearby.

Seaghdha spots him first.

Evander attempts to flee.

The party catches and restrains him.

---

## 3. The Order

Evander reveals that he has served the **Order of the Waning Veil**
since birth.

His mission was to obtain the Veil.

He claims the party is working for a heathen and does not understand
what is actually happening.

---

## 4. The Central Vampire

Evander explains that the Order has a central figure.

The figure:

-   is a vampire
-   has no known public name
-   appears to outrank most members of the Order
-   is angry at the party for killing so many vampires

Evander also suggests that some of the vampires were more compassionate
than others.

---

## 5. Ascension

The Order's purpose involves some form of ascension.

Evander does not understand the entire process.

He states:

> "We intend to win the war, this time."

He also expresses a desire to cease being human.

---

## 6. Eldros and the Order

Evander describes Eldros as a heathen who abandoned the Order.

He claims Eldros considers himself powerful and claims descent from the
Last Vampire.

The party learns the name:

**Vaelroth Valgrim, the Pale Ascendant.**

The similarity between Vaelroth's name and Eldros Valgrim's name becomes
significant.

---

## 7. Leonora

Evander has a personal relationship with **Leonora**.

Despite his loyalty to the Order, he insists that she is not involved.

The party obtains a final letter intended for Leonora.

Evander is killed before he can finish explaining what the Order is
doing.

---

## 8. The Order's Protocol

Evidence found on Evander includes a message:

> "If ever you get caught, be sure to remember the protocol. End
> yourself."

The message demonstrates how seriously the Order protects its secrets.

---

## 9. Eldros Arrives

Eldros comes for the Veil.

He creates a barrier to prevent the conversation from being overheard.

He makes a major revelation.

### Eldros's Lineage

Eldros confirms:

**Vaelroth Valgrim is his father.**

He intends to:

-   break Vaelroth's seal
-   purge the Order of the Waning Veil
-   restore what he considers the natural order

---

## 10. The Natural Order

Eldros explains that the original sealing of the Last Vampire was
intended to save society.

He believes the opposite occurred.

The sealing corrupted the balance of society.

His objective is to restore a natural predator and prey hierarchy.

### Eldros's Ideal

-   Vampires are predators.
-   Humans are prey.
-   Vampires should hunt and feed on humans.
-   Humans should not be enslaved or maintained as a servant population.

This places Eldros in direct ideological conflict with the Order of the
Waning Veil.

---

## 11. The Ascended

Eldros explains that the Ascendants exist outside the Material Plane.

There is a rite of ascension.

If an Ascendant is killed, the person responsible may replace that
deity.

The position does not necessarily have to be filled.

This reveals a larger supernatural structure behind the current
religious system.

---

## 12. The Veil Changes Hands

Seaghdha proposes keeping the Veil within the party.

Eldros refuses.

He reminds Seaghdha that he has already given him considerable freedom
and expects him to follow orders.

Seaghdha and Exanima ultimately give the Veil to Eldros.

---

## 13. The Blood Token

Eldros gives Seaghdha a token made from solid blood.

The token contains Eldros's blood.

Consuming it advances Seaghdha within the vampire hierarchy and grants
additional power.

Seaghdha consumes it.

---

## 14. Escape from Soulstone

The party leaves by caravan.

The city is under heavier guard.

Rumors circulate about the disappearance of the Veil and the possibility
of a new doctrine.

The party escapes the immediate reach of Soulstone.

---

## 15. Mitchell Gravelfoot

The party encounters **Mitchell Gravelfoot**, a big-game hunter.

He provides horses and warns the party about:

-   a man looking for Mal
-   a possible duel
-   a bald man seen traveling ahead
-   a long stretch before the next safe haven

---

## 16. Ranar Colt

The party encounters **Ranar Colt**, who knows Mal.

Ranar challenges Mal to a duel.

Mal kills him with a single shot.

Seaghdha drains Ranar's blood.

---

## 17. The Destroyed Campsite

The party discovers a torn campsite.

Evidence includes:

-   claw marks
-   a short sword
-   a journal mentioning Thralls
-   no obvious blood trail
-   no drag marks
-   an unusually clean campsite

The scene does not resemble a normal fight.

The party suspects an airborne creature may have been responsible.

## Key Revelations

-   The Order of the Waning Veil has a central vampire.
-   The Order seeks some form of ascension.
-   Evander was born into the Order.
-   Eldros is Vaelroth Valgrim's son.
-   Eldros wants to break Vaelroth's seal.
-   Eldros wants to purge the Waning Veil.
-   Eldros's idea of natural order is predator and prey, not human
    enslavement.
-   Ascendants can potentially be replaced through a rite of ascension.
-   Seaghdha's bloodline rank increases after consuming Eldros's blood.
-   The party leaves Soulstone.
