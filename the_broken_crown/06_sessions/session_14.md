# Session 14 — The Eight Runes

---

## At a glance

  ------------ ---------------------------------------
  **Played**   Notes recorded 2026-08/2026-09 period
  **Where**    Soulstone, ritual arena, Soul Cairn
  ------------ ---------------------------------------

The Night of Lanterns descends into a supernatural confrontation. The
party is transported into an arena-like space governed by eight runes,
encounters a powerful Church representative, and returns to Soulstone
with a demand from the Church regarding the Pale Ascendant Veil.

---

## 1. Panic

Civilians panic as the situation deteriorates.

A blue wall prevents normal access.

The party experiences the sensation of falling.

---

## 2. The Arena

The party is transported to an arena-like environment.

Devon becomes separated from the others.

The area contains:

-   Church figures
-   eight runes
-   a central priest
-   concentrated magical energy

---

## 3. The First Shepherd

A powerful figure identified as the **First Shepherd** appears.

The encounter involves:

-   eldritch magic
-   teleportation
-   lightning
-   Church ritual magic

---

## 4. The Priestess

A priestess creates a bone greatsword from her own body.

The weapon demonstrates the extreme nature of the magic being used.

---

## 5. The Eight Runes

Eight runes surround the Soulstone.

The entrances appear especially important.

The symbols resemble the ritual architecture encountered elsewhere.

The party begins to suspect that the arena, Soulstone, and other ritual
sites may share an underlying structure.

---

## 6. The Confrontation

The party fights through the encounter and eventually returns to the
chapel.

The Church becomes increasingly suspicious of Eldros.

---

## 7. Eldros and the Last Vampire

Church authorities suspect that Eldros is directly descended from the
**Last Vampire**.

This aligns with information already emerging from the vampire
investigations.

---

## 8. The Veil

The Church demands the return of the **Pale Ascendant Veil**.

The party is warned that refusal may result in:

-   excommunication
-   bounty
-   further Church hostility

Seaghdha questions whether the Veil is authentic.

---

## 9. The Pope

The Pope reveals a skeletal hand.

The discovery raises further questions regarding the Church's own
relationship with death and the supernatural.

---

## 10. Eggbert

Elliot befriends a corgi.

The corgi is named **Eggbert**.

---

## 11. Merol and the Veil

Merol escapes with the Veil.

The party receives compensation for the events surrounding the ceremony.

### Compensation

**1,150 Crowns**

---

## 12. Level 7

The party reaches **Level 7**.
