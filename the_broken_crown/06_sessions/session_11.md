# Session 11 — The Games Begin

[← Session 10](session_10.md) · [Session ledger](session_ledger.md) · [Session 12 →](session_12.md)

---

## At a glance

  ------------ ---------------------------------------
  **Played**   Notes recorded 2026-07/2026-08 period
  **Where**    Soulstone, sewers, vampire lair
  ------------ ---------------------------------------

The party discovers that several vampire groups are competing for the
same ancient relics. Eldros begins communicating directly with Devon,
Nymera's personal connections become clearer, and a mysterious vampire
announces that the games between the vampires have begun.

---

## 1. The Grand Archivist

The Grand Archivist warns Exanima not to contact them unless an
emergency occurs.

The warning suggests that Exanima's origins and the Iron Guardians
remain sensitive information.

---

## 2. Mal

The party encounters **Mal**, a bounty hunter carrying a revolver.

His history connects him to the Ravenwatch gunsmith tradition.

---

## 3. Eldros and Devon

Devon encounters **Eldros**.

Eldros appears taller and more commanding than before.

He explains that two vampires are working together while another woman
is manipulating events.

Eldros gives Devon a vial of his blood.

### The Blood Pact

Devon accepts a temporary blood pact.

The pact allows limited mental communication between them.

---

## 4. Loretta's Route

**Loretta** opens a route through the sewers.

The passage requires a sacrifice of her arm.

An earthquake strikes during the sequence.

The recurring connection between supernatural activity and seismic
disturbances becomes increasingly difficult to ignore.

---

## 5. Erendor

Erendor begins to undergo a more severe transformation.

The party continues to search for ways to preserve his identity while
dealing with his lycanthropy.

---

## 6. The Scarlet Bramble Ritual

The party discovers another hidden ritual site.

The chamber contains:

-   Scarlet Bramble Crown
-   Nightbloom
-   ritual components
-   vampiric activity

The party encounters vampires who offer an alliance.

### The Offer

The vampires propose working together against the Churches.

Their larger goal is to obtain the **Pale Ascendant relic**.

---

## 7. Tasha

**Tasha** is revealed to be a Thrall.

Her connection to Nymera becomes important.

Nymera is visibly affected when Tasha dies.

This establishes that some vampires maintain genuine emotional
attachments despite their predatory nature.

---

## 8. Nymera's Possessions

Nymera's belongings contain further evidence of the Great Houses.

Among them are:

-   Celestial correspondence
-   Ashcombe insignia
-   Greifenhald material

The party continues to uncover evidence that the vampire conflict
extends into noble politics.

---

## 9. Exanima's Reaction

Exanima absorbs the energy of the ritual.

The response mirrors previous interactions between Exanima, ancient
ritual circles, and Crown-era artifacts.

---

## 10. The Horn

Mal takes one of Nymera's horns.

The party leaves the encounter with additional evidence and trophies
from the vampire conflict.

---

## 11. The Translucent Vampire

A mysterious translucent vampire appears.

The figure tells the party:

> "The games between the vampires has begun."

The vampire promises that they will meet again during the **Night of
Lanterns**.

The statement reframes the vampire activity as part of a larger conflict
rather than a collection of isolated attacks.

---

## 12. The Party Advances

The party reaches **Level 6**.
