# Campaign State After Session 17

## The Vampire Hierarchy

The current known bloodline is:

**Serah'Kiel, the Crimson Seraph**\
↓\
**Vaelroth Valgrim, the Pale Ascendant**\
↓\
**Eldros Valgrim**\
↓\
**Seaghdha, Eldros's childe**

Separately, other vampire traditions and New Blood lineages exist,
including those associated with **Nerscylla**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Eldros's Position

Eldros's objectives are now substantially clearer.

He intends to:

-   break Vaelroth's seal
-   destroy or purge the Order of the Waning Veil
-   restore what he considers the natural order
-   preserve the predator/prey relationship between vampires and humans

His ideal order does not involve turning humanity into an enslaved
population.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## The Waning Veil

The Order:

-   has existed for generations
-   seeks some form of ascension
-   possesses a central vampire figure
-   has infiltrated human institutions
-   has manipulated House Ashcombe
-   turned Lord Regent Ashcombe into a Thrall
-   views Eldros as a traitor or heathen
-   wants the Pale Ascendant and related relics

The Order's ultimate ascension plan remains incomplete.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Seaghdha's Transformation

Seaghdha is now firmly established as Eldros's childe.

His transformation continues to manifest physically.

Known changes include:

-   pale blue-white feathers
-   coloration resembling Eldros's hair
-   increased vampiric abilities
-   greater position within Eldros's hierarchy after consuming Eldros's
    blood

His connection to the Scarlet Bramble remains active.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## The Crimson Seraph

The identity of the **Crimson Seraph** is now known as:

**Serah'Kiel.**

The Crimson Seraph appears to predate the modern concept of vampires.

The Black Tithe is associated with Serah'Kiel and teaches that life
creates a debt to the world.

The current evidence points toward Serah'Kiel as the origin of Eldros
and Vaelroth's bloodline.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## The Griffin

The Griffin investigation remains unresolved.

Known facts:

-   The creature is unusually large.
-   Griffins were more common in ancient times.
-   The creature attacked missionaries.
-   Allisia is investigating it.
-   Navanhi is hunting it.
-   Evidence suggests Demerin involvement.
-   The region contains unusual underground spaces and ancient
    monuments.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## The Crowned Witness

The Crowned Witness has resurfaced through fragments of forgotten
history.

The roadside monument suggests that:

-   the religion once had a significant following
-   some Ravencrofts still secretly honor it
-   its principles influenced Ravencroft's ideas of order and justice
-   forgotten religions may survive through memory even when formal
    worship disappears
