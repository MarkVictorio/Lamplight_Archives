# Session 5 — Soulstone, The Gallows & Eldros Pact

[← Session 4](session_04.md) · [Session ledger](session_ledger.md)

---

## At a glance

| | |
|:---|:---|
| **What happened** | The party enters Soulstone and uncovers an **Eldros-linked supply operation**, encounters **memory inconsistencies (Alya)**, witnesses a **public Thrall execution**, and intervenes to free **Sigmar**. During the night, **Seaghdha performs a ritual using the red gemstone**, invoking **Eldros Valgrim** and becoming his **first bound Thrall**. |
| **Where** | Sewers → Iron Hand Tavern → Gallows → Greyhold → outskirts |
| **Status** | Escalation — public instability and player transformation |
| **Involved** | Party · **Eldros Valgrim** · Bram Blackthorne · Sigmar · Alya (uncertain) · Lothar Thorneveil |

This session marks the transition from hidden disturbances to **visible systemic breakdown**, alongside the first instance of a player entering the system as a **bound entity**.

---

## 1. Sewer activity — Eldros’ logistics

- Above a manhole, the party hears **crates**, **barrels**, and active transport.
- Workers discuss **military surplus** and **potions**.
- Shipment details:
  - Value: **2,500 crowns**
  - Destination: **Iron Hand Tavern**
  - Departure: **sunrise**
- The city remains unnaturally dark: **no lamps lit**, **moon obscured**.

**Action**

- Seaghdha steals **2 healing potions**.
- A manifest ties the shipment to **Eldros**.

**Read**

- Eldros operates **material logistics**, not just isolated supernatural appearances.
- The operation suggests planning, distribution, and wider influence.

---

## 2. Iron Hand Tavern — social landscape

- The **Iron Hand Tavern** is the oldest tavern in Soulstone.
- Its architecture mirrors the Lamplight tavern, only larger.

**Atmosphere**

- Thick smoke, crowded rooms.
- Patrons show signs of **severe sleep deprivation**.
- Wealthy nobles mix with common drinkers.

**Religion**

- The region is dominated by the **Pale Shepherd**.
- **Last Ember** clergy are rare, so **Ansel** stands out immediately.

---

## 3. Nobility — Corvin & Ulric

- The party meets **Magistrate Corvin** and **Ulric**.

**Behavior**

- They call the party “peculiar.”
- They attempt to **purchase Seaghdha** while he is wildshaped.
- Their offer rises to **650 crowns**.
- They even produce an **arcane locking cage**.

**Outcome**

- The party avoids escalation.

**Read**

- Soulstone nobility project wealth and detachment while treating people as property.
- The exchange reinforces the city’s polished but unstable tone.

---

## 4. Public notice — the gallows

Notice board reads:

> “Tomorrow at 8am, be at the Gallows.  
> See three Thralls be hanged.  
> The Greifenhalds are not to be messed with.  
> Soulstone is safe.”

**Read**

- The execution is staged as both **public reassurance** and a **political demonstration of control**.

---

## 5. Tavern leads & context

- Bartender: **Ka’el**
- He has a **metallic hand** and seems ex-military.
- He is hesitant to speak openly.

**Information gathered**

- Three Thralls were captured from **mausoleum disturbances**.
- Reports mention **undug graves** and **moving structures**.
- The execution will occur in the **city center**.
- The party rents a room in what used to be **former barracks**.

---

## 6. Alya — unstable presence

- A woman named **Alya** approaches **Ansel**.
- She questions his motives and comes across as perceptive, probing, and slightly unfocused, as if looking past him.
- She leaves a **hidden note**.

**Later**

- She provides a **5-use Sending scroll**.
- She says she seeks **justice** and that Soulstone’s problems are **her concern**.
- She disappears immediately after.

**Contradiction**

- **Ka’el** claims no such woman was ever present.

**Read**

- The scene suggests a memory or perception inconsistency.
- Alya may exist outside normal perception, be selectively visible, or tie into a larger systemic failure.

---

## 7. Residual phenomena

- **Devon** notices blood giving off faint, smoke-like trails.
- Seaghdha’s **red gemstone** emits warmth and reacts to changing light.

**Read**

- Magic here feels residual, trackable, and still active after the main event.

---

## 8. The ritual — Eldros pact

During the night, Seaghdha uses the **red gemstone**.

**Invocation**

- Seaghdha calls upon **Eldros Valgrim**.
- The ritual aligns with **New Blood phenomena**.

**Result**

- Seaghdha enters a **binding contract**.
- He becomes **Eldros’s Thrall**.
- His soul is **bound directly to Eldros**.

**Nature**

- Voluntary
- Permanent
- Not coffin-based

This is the first known instance of a **player-initiated Thrall creation**.

---

## 9. Eldros encounter — night

- Eldros appears beside Devon
- Presence marked by cold

**Statements**

- He says he will be seen **“in the crowd”** at sunrise.
- He mentions **Misthaven**.
- He advises the party to attend a **ball**.
- He leaves a **gift via Ka’el** under the code **Gareck**.

**Read**

- Eldros moves freely, anticipates events, and seems socially embedded rather than isolated.

---

## 10. Return of the sun

- Darkness lifts.
- The sun returns.
- The city celebrates.
- Drinks are offered freely.

**Tone**

- The relief feels real, but also slightly unnatural or forced.

---

## 11. Bram Blackthorne

- **Bram Blackthorne** appears as an emissary of the **Blackthorne** with ties to **Ravencroft**.
- He looks roughly **90 years old**, but physically closer to **50**, and is visibly scarred.

**Behavior**

- He is observant.
- He offers the party access to the gallows.

**Read**

- Law enforcement presence is escalating.
- Ravencroft is actively involved in Soulstone affairs.

---

## 12. The gallows

- Three prisoners are presented.
- One of them is identified as **Sigmar**.

**Signs**

- Pale
- Aggressive
- Attempts to bite, suggesting possible **Thrall** state

**Warning**

- **Ansel** receives a message not to interfere.

**Execution**

- All three are hanged.

**After**

- The bodies are scheduled to be burned in **3 days**.

---

## 13. Intervention

- Seaghdha disrupts the execution with **fog**, destroys the **rope**, and frees **Sigmar**.

**Chaos**

- The crowd panics.
- Guards respond.
- **Yer-min** appears, seems familiar with **Devon**, and encourages action.
- **Plagueis** is arrested.

---

## 14. Escape

- Seaghdha shrinks **Sigmar** and escapes by flight.
- **Bram** attempts an interception.
- A **raven** watches, then departs.

---

## 15. Greyhold

- The party is detained for questioning.
- A **Greywarden** recognizes their prior pardon and reviews the Lamplight connection.

**Outcome**

- The party is released with a warning.
- An official record is established.

---

## 16. Thorneveil thread

- The party encounters performer **Lothar Thorneveil**.

**Interaction**

- He denies knowledge of **Seris**.
- **Ansel** detects deception.

**Outcome**

- The party is detained again.
- Bail is set at **500 crowns**.

---

## 17. Splinter — Sigmar’s escape

- Seaghdha escorts **Sigmar** away from the city.

**Actions**

- Hair is cut.
- A disguise is applied.
- **Disguise Self** scrolls are used.

**Plan**

- Travel is arranged toward the **Scarlet Bramble** or **Iron-Mire Valley**.

**Outcome**

- Smuggling is arranged.
- Seaghdha’s bounty is cleared the next day.

---

## 18. Gains & status

- **Devon** ends with **4 potions** and **100 crowns total**.
- The party is now **recorded by authorities** and **under observation**.
