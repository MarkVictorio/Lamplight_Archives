## 1. The camp and Limuelle

Near the camp: a **fresh corpse**; a **golem** seems to have caught the victim by surprise.

**On the body**

- A leatherette **journal** — *“Journal belonging to Limuelle.”*
- Limuelle was traveling to Sunstone to *“finally meet his love in Sunstone.”* The lover’s name is **Rose**.
- A **dried rose** in a woven satchel.
- About **one day’s rations** for three, a **bedroll**, **firewood**.

*(Misc. table beats: Sheya, Plagueis, Exanima, Devon — per original notes.)*

---

## 2. The sky and the gate

- The **sun does not rise** when it should. A timepiece reads about **1 p.m.**, but the sky still feels like **evening**.
- Only a **blood-red moon** — **no documented precedent** in the current era; folk have forgotten it was ever a thing.
- **Howls** come from the **east**.

The party takes **two horses** and leaves the **carriage** behind.

**Sunstone**

- Guards at the entrance.
- A small (~4 ft) **lizardfolk** figure waits — **Rose**, Limuelle’s fiancée.
- **Cover story:** Limuelle died bravely fighting a golem / giant python. Rose asks for his **scarf**; Seagh **retrieves** it.

---

## 3. Town and Seed Sip Inn

**Church**  
A **new church** in Sunstone is dedicated to the **Last Ember**.

**The pilgrim**

- From **Lamplight**, arrived about a **week** ago.
- **Antisocial** — speaks to no one, **boarded in his room five days**.
- Ledger name: **Cliff**. Described as a **balding man in his sixties** (later, the **real** Cliff does not match **Eldros** appearing **handsome** in **Room 301**).

**Sigmar**

- On the inn ledger.
- **Rumor:** unfaithful to his prior faith, drawn to the **Last Ember**.

**Tone of the town**

- Sunstone is **chiefly Ashcomb** territory; trade concentrates in the center.
- People seem **too relaxed**. News from the capital may lag **a day or two**.

**Seed Sip Inn**

- Patron **Stern** — **Triton** with a **prosthetic arm**; uncomfortable around Devon.
- Cliff ordered **grits**, to be brought to **Room 301** (attic). He **prepaid fifteen days**.

**Split (in-world plan)**  
Some PCs hit the inn (Cliff, Sigmar); others blacksmith / Church. Reconvene at the inn around **5:20 p.m.** (from **2:20 p.m.**).

## 4. Cliffhanger — Room 301

The room is **pitch black**. A **silhouette** faces away.

**Eldros** appears as the **handsome man** and **isolates Devon** — session ends on that beat.
