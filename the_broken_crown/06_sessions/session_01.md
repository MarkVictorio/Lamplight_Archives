The party answers a desperate letter and finds a city going through the motions: hollow commerce, broken justice imagery, and a basement ritual tied to Crown-era **Thralls**. They meet **Sigmar**, trace **House Ashcomb**, and leave with proof the **seal below** is in trouble.

---

## 1. The letter and the square

A message attributed to [DM] warns that Lamplight has changed: something is wrong in the gathering square and worse in the streets. The lamps burn, but no one remembers lighting them. The Ember is said to be dimming. Men have vanished, records burned, names rewritten. The writer begs the reader to come quietly and ask for **Sigmar** at the central light — the city’s trading square.

In the square, merchants mimic work without substance. Flowers are wilted. Folk say:

- *“Order restores the path.”*
- *“The scales have broken.”*

On the butcher’s board, a **judgment scale** is carved **cut in half at the stem**. The butcher collapses; another passerby ducks into a room and slams the door. When the butcher wakes, he chops at empty air.

---

## 2. Sigmar’s Oddities

The party finds **Sigmar’s Oddities** — conventional magic trinkets and stranger stock.

They interrupt an attack: a blonde man lies in his own blood; an open window; bloody footprints; a hooded figure flees. They stop the assailant and help the victim.

- The man is **Sigmar**. He received the same kind of letter.
- The method matches what they hear of **House Ashcomb**.
- Sigmar wore a protective **necklace**; something changed overnight, but it may have saved him.
- He intends to flee toward the capital and has ties to **House Ravencroft**.
- He gives each PC **one potion of healing** and points them at the tavern where the Ashcombs are holed up.

On a body tied to the house: **Ashcomb sigil**, a **rusted knife**, and a scrap of note:

> *…wash away the stain of justice denied, close the eye to doubt, and let the Last Ember take what remains.*

---

## 3. Central Light and the tavern

More people appear in the streets. The group toys with posing as Ashcomb.

At **Central Light**, townsfolk **walk in a circle**. When **Seagh** snuffs a lamp, they stare; afterward, merchants and crowd drift home. Someone asks whether the butcher is homeless. The **ground trembles**; lights inside the tavern **go out**.

Inside the tavern:

- **Two corpses** — the same pair who earlier asked the party to **relight the lamps**.
- The place seemed fine about a day ago. Food and drink are still edible, not rotted.
- The **tavern keeper** lies unconscious behind the bar. A faint **blood trail** leads toward the wash area.

---

## 4. Washroom and basement

**Basins** sit under the wash area. Each has **religious symbolism** and its own drain:

| Basin | Symbol | Note |
|:------|:--------|:-----|
| One | Last Ember | — |
| One | Smooth eye | — |
| One | Broken scale | **Blood** here |

The bartender and the **middle tap** carry a **necrotic** smell.

**Basement**

- A magical **awakening circle**, dead members of **House Ashcomb**, and a **closed coffin**.
- Papers describe **Thralls** from the time of the Crown — beings tied to their coffins. Two awakened examples were weakened near **sunstone**.
- This coffin and a summoner’s journal document an attempt to revive a Thrall. A **shattered blade** lies in the circle.
- Table consensus: the town’s wrongness stems from this ritual waking the Thrall.
- **GM tag:** Sir Garran Holt.

**Tablet (broken stone)**  
Inscription: *“The Seal below is failing.”*

The party sets course for **Sunstone**.
