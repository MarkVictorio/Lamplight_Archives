# Session 16 — The Griffin Road

[← Session 15](session_15.md) · [Session ledger](session_ledger.md) · [Session 17 →](session_17.md)

---

## At a glance

  ------------ -------------------------------
  **Played**   18--19 September 2026 (notes)
  **Where**    Road toward Ebonshard Mines
  ------------ -------------------------------

The party travels toward Ebonshard Mines and encounters signs of a large
Griffin, trained goblins, an ancient religious monument, and a wounded
Last Ember priest. Seaghdha's physical transformation continues, while a
vision reveals an ancient connection between the Crimson Seraph and the
origin of his bloodline.

---

## 1. Smoke Ahead

The party is warned about smoke a day's ride ahead.

The smoke appears to originate near the **Ebonshard Mines**.

---

## 2. The Griffin

Mitchell reports seeing a huge flying creature.

Descriptions include:

-   roughly a quarter the size of Castle Greifenhald
-   feathered
-   blue and orange coloring
-   large enough to resemble an inn-sized bird in local rumors

Devon recognizes the creature as a **Griffin**.

Historically, Griffins were more common and some possessed magical
abilities.

---

## 3. Mitchell's Injury

Mitchell appears to favor one leg.

He is also connected to a butchershop in Soulstone.

Rumors from Soulstone include a theft at the Church of the Pale
Shepherd.

---

## 4. The Roadside Monument

Exanima scouts ahead and gives Devon an **Ashcombe family signet**.

The party later reaches a rest stop containing:

-   a large hero statue
-   smaller statues built into the mountain
-   radiant energy
-   glyphs resembling the arena
-   Pale Shepherd symbols

---

## 5. The Hidden Hole

Eggbert reacts violently to the bushes.

Erendor discovers a small hole leading into the mountain.

Tracks indicate that creatures have been squeezing in and out.

The party decides to seal the entrance with a boulder.

---

## 6. The Crowned Witness

Devon recognizes one of the statues as the **Ascendant of Order**,
associated with the **Crowned Witness**.

The religion is no longer widely practiced.

Merol remembers that some Ravencrofts secretly hold the Ascendant dear.

The old principle is:

> "Nothing in history truly disappears so long as someone remembers it."

The discovery reinforces the hidden religious history surrounding the
Great Houses.

---

## 7. The Missing Campers

The nearby tents appear recently abandoned.

However:

-   there are no footsteps leaving the site
-   the fire is still relatively fresh
-   there are no obvious signs of departure

It appears that the people were simply plucked away.

---

## 8. Goblin Scouts

Seaghdha flies above the road and discovers goblin scouts watching the
horses.

The goblins call him:

> "The bad feathered one."

More goblins appear near the road.

---

## 9. Trained Goblins

The goblins possess equipment unusual for ordinary goblin groups.

They have:

-   iron and steel weapons
-   grenades
-   organized scouting behavior

A dying goblin warns:

> "Radagar will come for you."

The identity of Radagar remains unknown.

---

## 10. The Wounded Priest

The party discovers a wounded priest of the **Last Ember**.

They recover:

-   3 makeshift bombs
-   107 Crowns
-   a Wand of Fireballs with four charges
-   a Last Ember religious symbol

Seaghdha heals the priest.

---

## 11. Allisia

The priest identifies Seaghdha as a member of the **Scarlet Bramble**.

He also reports seeing a young Tabaxi druid named **Allisia**.

Description:

-   Tabaxi
-   snow-leopard-like appearance
-   young and relatively inexperienced
-   blunt personality

She is traveling toward Ebonshard to investigate the Griffin.

---

## 12. The Missing Missionaries

The priest explains that he and his companions were missionaries sent
from Sunstone toward the Central Spire.

Two companions, **Akiva** and **Magnus**, were taken by the Griffin.

Akiva was likely taken alive.

Magnus may have died.

The priest also knows **Ansel** and believes Ansel may have traveled
toward the Central Spire with the High Keeper and Belladonna.

---

## 13. The Man Beneath the Tree

During the night's watch, Merol encounters a strange man leaning against
a tree.

Several ravens sit in the branches.

The man:

-   appears calm
-   watches Merol
-   beckons him closer

The information exchanged remains unresolved.

---

## 14. Seaghdha Changes

Seaghdha undergoes further physical changes.

His feathers become:

-   bluish
-   pale white
-   glacier-like

The color resembles **Eldros's hair**.

---

## 15. Lothar's Carriage

Seaghdha spots a carriage carrying two people.

The driver is **Lothar**.

The carriage is traveling toward **Lion's Mane**.

---

## 16. The Crimson Seraph

Seaghdha experiences a vision of **Nerscylla** before her vampirism.

She appears with:

-   pale skin
-   black feathered wings
-   a halo-like crown of thorns

The vision raises the name:

**Crimson Seraph.**

---

## 17. The Black Tithe

Devon recognizes the Crimson Seraph as **Serah'Kiel**.

The figure is associated with the ancient **Black Tithe** religion.

The Black Tithe predates the modern term "vampire."

Its central belief is that:

-   every life must eventually be paid back to the world
-   death is a debt rather than an end

The religion was largely outlawed.

---

## 18. The True Bloodline

The vision establishes an important distinction.

Eldros's bloodline does **not** originate from Nerscylla.

Neither Eldros nor the Pale Ascendant were created through Nerscylla's
blood.

The vision instead points toward the **Crimson Seraph** as the origin
point of their bloodline.

---

## 19. The Red-Eyed Raven

A raven with a scarlet ruby eye watches Devon and Seaghdha.

The bird disappears as dawn approaches.

Its purpose remains unknown.

---

## 20. The Cinder Synod

A white-haired woman escorted by armored figures approaches the camp.

She identifies herself as part of the **Cinder Synod**, the High
Keeper's innermost circle.

She asks whether the party follows the Last Ember.

After hearing the wounded priest's story, she gives the party a personal
debt payment.

## Key Revelations

-   The Ebonshard region is being affected by a large Griffin.
-   Goblins in the region appear organized and unusually well equipped.
-   The Crowned Witness survives only through fragments of memory.
-   Some Ravencrofts secretly honor the Crowned Witness.
-   Seaghdha's body continues to change after becoming Eldros's childe.
-   Nerscylla was apparently not always a vampire.
-   The Crimson Seraph is identified as Serah'Kiel.
-   The Black Tithe predates the modern concept of vampirism.
-   Eldros and the Pale Ascendant do not descend from Nerscylla.
-   Their bloodline instead points toward the Crimson Seraph.
-   A mysterious red-eyed raven continues to observe the party.
