# Session 12 — Another Life, Another Battle

## At a glance

  ------------ -----------------------------------------------
  **Played**   Notes recorded 2026-08 period
  **Where**    Soul Cairn, Iron Guardian ruins, vampire lair
  ------------ -----------------------------------------------

The party uncovers more of Exanima's ancient identity, confronts a
former brother-in-arms, and discovers Seaghdha undergoing a dangerous
vampiric transformation. Eldros intervenes and establishes Seaghdha's
place within his bloodline.

---

## 1. Funeral Rites

The party encounters a dead priest of the Pale Shepherd.

The body contains:

-   a stab wound
-   no blood trail

The circumstances suggest that the death may be connected to the ongoing
supernatural activity.

---

## 2. Iron Guardian Ruins

The party explores ancient Iron Guardian ruins.

Exanima recognizes another unit from his own era.

### Rubrik

Rubrik appears to belong to the same batch as Exanima.

The encounter reinforces the age of the Iron Guardians and the scale of
the forces that existed before modern Aaldharyn.

---

## 3. Calvin

Calvin's notes are discovered.

The party eventually confronts him.

The Grand Archivist kills Calvin using a green bolt.

The event creates further uncertainty regarding the motives and
authority of the Grand Archivist.

---

## 4. Gawain Lionheart

The party discovers that **Gawain Lionheart** was once Exanima's
brother-in-arms.

Exanima remembers:

> "Arcthurius Lioneye."

The confrontation becomes personal.

Gawain is defeated.

Before his death, he tells Exanima:

> "I will wait for another battle in another life, brother."

---

## 5. Proto-King's Mercy

Gawain's greatsword transforms into a weapon identified as
**Proto-King's Mercy**.

The weapon connects the present conflict to the much older history of
the King's Mercy.

---

## 6. The Vampire Lair

The party discovers Seaghdha in the middle of a transformation.

**Nerella Voss** is present and dies during the confrontation.

The situation becomes increasingly dangerous as Seaghdha's condition
progresses.

---

## 7. Eldros Arrives

Eldros reveals that Seaghdha can still be saved.

He explains that the transformation is not simply an ordinary Thrall
condition.

Eldros performs a ritual to stabilize the process.

---

## 8. Seaghdha's Bloodline

Eldros formally claims Seaghdha within his vampire lineage.

### Sire and Childe

Seaghdha is established as **Eldros's childe**.

The relationship is a vampire **sire → childe** bond.

This distinction is important:

-   Seaghdha is not merely a generic Thrall.
-   Eldros is his sire.
-   Seaghdha's transformation places him within Eldros's bloodline.

---

## 9. Eldros's Authority

Eldros tells Seaghdha:

> "Remember your allegiance, Seagh."

The statement establishes the expectations attached to their
relationship.

Eldros's influence over Seaghdha is both personal and hierarchical.

---

## 10. Devon's Task

Eldros reminds Devon:

> "You are still to retrieve the cape."

The Pale Ascendant relic remains part of Eldros's broader objectives.
