## Running threads (as of Session 8)

- **Sigmar** survives the gallows and is smuggled out; **Seraphine** / seal / **Mistress of Val'wrath** status remains unclear.
- **Eldros** motives vs **New Bloods** / **Nerscylla**; he can now create Thralls **intentionally** through direct pact.
- **Seaghdha** is Eldros's first confirmed **voluntary / active Thrall**; soul-binding, control limits, and consequences remain unclear.
- **Alya** introduces a memory / perception anomaly; her nature and allegiance are unclear.
- **Erendor** — alive, forced lycanthrope, first apprentice of **Caldrick Vane**; partial control with **stabilization bracers**; purification possible only at **Argent Vale** (dangerous, possibly fatal).
- **Bald Demerin operative** — responsible for Sunstone kidnappings and experiments; **not** an official House Demerin representative; journal (New Blood rituals, **Nerscylla** contact) held by Seaghdha.
- **Ritual circles** — network linking sites (Lamplight, Sunstone chapel, ballroom floor); functions include **transport**, **resurrection** of Children of the Night, and **communion** with Nerscylla.
- **Vampire infiltration** — nobility compromised; female vampire escaped eastward; **Seraph Ashcombe** has no documented Ashcombe lineage (Chronicler **Maelis** found nothing).
- **King's Mercy** — Crown-era records recovered; predates first King; **diamond-shaped gemstone** still missing; vampiric dust required for reforging; linked to **Mad King** theories.
- **Exanima** — construction manual (Old Common), Orins bearing child likeness, absorbed ruby ritual construct; **Jorenfeld** (Demerin) calls him "defective," creating unintended memories; invited to **Achroma**.
- **Devon** — memories tie to lost kingdom **Oltheros** (hawk heraldry); homeland now beneath **Temples of the Ascended**; **General Sten Rowe** name surfaced.
- **Ansel** — path toward **Warden of Ash**; mission from **Halbet Rune** to investigate **Last Vampire** relics at **Festival of the Dead** (Soul Cairn, ~5 days); High Marshal opposes destruction.
- **Soul Cairn** — largest regional graveyard; two more **armories** known; Demerin researchers on site for at least a month; **Festival of the Dead** (~30–40 years old) displays Last Vampire relics.
- **Thrall anomaly** — Fighters' Guild member became Thrall **without** the usual earthquake; pattern may be changing.
- **Bram Blackthorne** — killed at ball, revived; reports peaceful **Silent Choir** afterlife experience.
- **House Ashcomb** (old vs new sigils), **Ravencroft**, **Argent Veil**, and **Blackthorne** pressure extend the political knot across Soulstone and the noble gathering.
