## Running threads (as of Session 17)



### Vampire hierarchy (confirmed at table)



**Serah'Kiel, the Crimson Seraph** → **Vaelroth Valgrim, the Pale Ascendant** → **Eldros Valgrim** → **Seaghdha** (sire/childe, not a generic Thrall). Separate traditions remain (**Nerscylla**, New Blood, etc.).



### Eldros



- Son of **Vaelroth**; aims to **break Vaelroth's seal**, **purge the Order of the Waning Veil**, restore predator/prey order (not mass human enslavement).

- Holds the **Pale Ascendant Veil**; gave Seaghdha a **solid blood token** (consumed).

- **Devon** owes retrieval of a **cape** (Pale Ascendant relic thread); temporary **blood pact** for limited mental contact.

- Church suspects Eldros descends from the **Last Vampire** ([session 14](session_14.md)).



### Order of the Waning Veil



- Evander (**Evander Holyfield**) born into the Order; sought the Veil; killed when captured ([session 15](session_15.md)).

- Central **unnamed vampire**; ascension agenda — details incomplete.

- **Lord Regent Ashcombe** turned **Thrall** on Order orders ([session 17](session_17.md)).

- Evander linked to **Leonora**; package exchange with Order observed earlier ([session 10](session_10.md)).



### Seaghdha



- **Crown of Scarlet Bramble** taken by **Nymera**, then further Bramble regalia (**Crown of Thorns**, **Nightbloom**) after [session 13](session_13.md).

- Physical change: pale blue-white feathers like Eldros's hair; flight; Scarlet Bramble recognition on the road.

- **Ravencroft** spies reported identity to **Spymaster Velor** ([session 10](session_10.md)).



### Relics & artifacts



- **King's Mercy** line: **Proto-King's Mercy** from **Gawain Lionheart** ([session 12](session_12.md)).

- **Pale Ascendant** relic set: Shroud (purified red → white), **Pale White Lantern**, Veil (enchanted: flight, invisibility, bat form).

- **Exanima**: Demerin upgrades; recognized as **Iron Guardian** in Soul Cairn; **Rubrik**, **Failsafe** (Third Fleet); history with **Gawain** / **Arcthurius Lioneye**.



### Ashcombe



- **Seraph**: foundling, not blood Ashcombe; bird bones, magic interest ([session 17](session_17.md)).

- **Lady Elira** missing ~two weeks since the ball.

- **Lucius Ashcombe** captured and released (hand severed as warning).

- Lord Regent reopened silver mines, sold armaments; strongest lineage through **mother's side**.



### Devon / Oltheros



- **Soul Fountain** memories: acropolis, childhood home ([session 09](session_09.md)).

- **500 Orins**, military note **General Sten Rowe**, cloak buttons match his gear.

- **Ashcombe family signet** from Exanima ([session 16](session_16.md)); recognizes **Serah'Kiel** / **Black Tithe** ([session 16](session_16.md)).



### Party travel & threats



- Left **Soulstone** by caravan ([session 15](session_15.md)); road toward **Ebonshard Mines** / Griffin territory.

- **Griffin**: huge, blue-orange; missionaries **Akiva** / **Magnus** taken; **Allisia** (Tabaxi druid) investigating; **Navanhi** (Iron Mire) hunting for chieftainship.

- Organized **goblins** (**Radagar** named); **Demerin** may have provoked the Griffin.

- **Sigmar**: malformed Thrall without master → ritual → **newborn vampire** ([session 17](session_17.md)).

- Authorities increasingly suspicious of party undead ties.



### Ansel (split party)



- Wounded Last Ember priest knows **Ansel**; believed Ansel traveled toward **Central Spire** with **High Keeper Helvic** and **Belladonna Thornveil** ([session 16](session_16.md)).



### Unresolved (high priority)



- Waning Veil central vampire's identity; full ascension plan; beyond **Vaelroth's seal**.

- **Lady Elira**, **Seraph**'s true origin, bird bones.

- **Red-eyed raven**, man beneath the tree (Merol's watch), **Radagar**, Ebonshard smoke.

- **Galamar** / **Seven Swords** and Navanhi's quest.

- **Night of Lanterns** translucent vampire; female vampire escaped east ([session 08](session_08.md)).

- Earthquake vs non-earthquake Thrall awakenings.
