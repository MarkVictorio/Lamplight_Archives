|            |                                                       |
| :--------- | :---------------------------------------------------- |

The sun fails to rise again. Investigating strange activity beneath Sunstone, the party uncovers a hidden facility connected to lycanthropic experimentation, Crown-era relics, Exanima research, and a captive Argent Veil apprentice transformed against his will.

---

## 1. Sunstone in Darkness

The unnatural night continues.

* The sun fails to rise.
* Tremors continue throughout the region.
* Bram Blackthorne believes vampires and lycanthropes may be cooperating based on historical precedent.
* Bram notes that earthquakes have historically accompanied the summoning of powerful vampires or Thralls.
* He has witnessed a man bearing House Demerin's sigil repeatedly entering the tavern basement carrying chains and eventually dragging a cage underground.
* Magistrate Corvin reports that Ulric has disappeared.
* Corvin shows clear hostility toward Elliot and the Argent Veil.
* Seaghda learns he is no longer considered a wanted man.

---

## 2. Return to the Sewers

Believing there is a connection between the tavern and the sewers, Elliot and Ansel return underground.

They discover:

* A hidden button concealed within the tunnels.
* Pressing it triggers mechanical movement within the walls.
* Muffled screams and wailing become audible.
* A magical hum resonates through the stone.

A concealed doorway opens.

---

## 3. The First Hidden Room

The newly revealed chamber appears abandoned.

Inside:

| Item                  | Notes            |
| :-------------------- | :--------------- |
| Chains                | Various sizes    |
| Manacles              | Taken by Devon   |
| Scroll of Hold Person | Taken by Seaghda |

The room contains damaged furniture and signs of long-term neglect.

Most furnishings are destroyed.

Only:

* A piano
* A mirror
* A dresser

remain intact.

A sheet of music rests on the piano.

Title:

**The Keys to the Kingdom**

When Devon performs the listed notes (G, C, and E), another hidden passage opens.

---

## 4. Shrine Beyond the Wall

Through a narrow opening, the party discovers another chamber.

Visible features include:

* A statue of the Pale Shepherd decorated with red roses.
* Two dormant automatons.
* Large quantities of scrap and debris.
* Multiple skeletons.

Seaghda transforms into a rat and slips through the crack.

Additional discoveries:

* Incomplete Exanima prototypes.
* A large lever.
* Evidence of experimentation and construction.

---

## 5. The Forge

The next chamber functions as a workshop and forge.

### Exanima Records

A chest contains approximately ten pages detailing the construction of Exanima.

### The King's Mercy

Another chest contains historical records regarding the artifact known as **The King's Mercy**.

### Ancient Dead

The forge contains numerous preserved corpses.

Investigation suggests:

* They are over one hundred years old.
* They were once master forgemasters.

---

## 6. The Chained Beast

Beyond another doorway, the party encounters a large wolf-like humanoid bound within astral chains and a magical circle.

Combat begins immediately.

### Battle Notes

* Ansel is grappled and downed.
* The creature is eventually subdued.
* The magical circle continues pulsing after the transformation recedes.

Seaghda heals the captive for questioning.

---

## 7. Erendor

The captive identifies himself as **Erendor**.

Important details:

* Speaks Elvish rather than Common.
* Former member of the Argent Veil.
* First apprentice of Sir Caldrick Vane.
* Ashamed of his condition.
* Considers himself impure.

### His Story

Erendor explains:

* He and Caldrick were on a scouting mission.
* Chains emerged from the mist and captured him.
* He became separated from Caldrick.
* Signs of lycanthropy manifested shortly afterward.

Initially, he mistakes Ansel for his captor due to his hooded appearance.

### Additional Revelations

Erendor reveals:

* Other captives exist.
* Many victims die during the process.
* Some become weaker forms of undead.
* A bald vampire is responsible.
* Additional prisoners remain nearby.

When Elliot promises to reveal the truth of what happened, Erendor refuses.

He believes death would be preferable to living as a monster.

---

## 8. Erendor's Breakdown

While discussing his fate, Erendor transforms again.

A second confrontation follows.

Cornered and overwhelmed by despair, Erendor attempts to kill himself with his own claws.

Elliot intervenes.

Erendor is subdued and rendered unconscious rather than slain.

---

## 9. The Prisoners

The party discovers several captives.

Three surviving peasants are freed from cages.

Their presence supports Erendor's claims regarding abductions and experimentation.

---

## 10. The Strategy Room

The final chamber appears to function as a command center and storage area.

### Equipment

| Item                 | Notes                          |
| :------------------- | :----------------------------- |
| Splint Mail          | AC 17, Strength 15 requirement |
| 1,000 Orins          | Pre-Crown currency             |
| 50 ft rope with hook | Recovered                      |
| Coats of arms        | Various                        |
| Crown-era maps       | Valuable historical records    |

### Crown of Scarlet Bramble

The party recovers an artifact:

**A crown of thorns woven from Scarlet Bramble.**

Seaghda recognizes imagery associated with the Scarlet Ascension but confirms it is not a holy symbol of his order.

### Ancient Maps

Recovered maps depict Aaldharyn before unification.

Contents include:

* Military campaigns.
* Historical settlements.
* Raid routes.
* Supply lines.
* Territorial boundaries.

These maps would be extremely valuable to historians and noble houses.

---

## 11. Devon's Memory

While studying the maps, Devon experiences a powerful reaction.

One location near Soulstone feels familiar despite not existing on modern maps.

A forgotten name surfaces:

**General Sten Rowe**

The title itself feels ancient.

Modern Aaldharyn uses **High Marshal** instead of **General**.

Devon briefly senses something reaching toward him through the memory before the feeling fades.

---

## 12. Silence

As the party finishes looting the strategy room, a realization settles over them.

The sounds that had echoed throughout the facility suddenly cease.

No screams.

No cries.

No voices.

Only silence remains.

The session ends with the party standing in the quiet aftermath of whatever occurred beneath Sunstone.

---

## Key Revelations

* The sun's disappearance continues.
* House Demerin appears connected to secret underground activity.
* A hidden facility beneath Sunstone was conducting lycanthropic experimentation.
* Crown-era Exanima schematics were recovered.
* Erendor, first apprentice of Caldrick Vane, was transformed against his will.
* A bald vampire appears responsible for multiple kidnappings and experiments.
* The party recovered 1,000 Orins and valuable Crown-era maps.
* Devon experienced memories connected to General Sten Rowe.
* A Scarlet Bramble crown was recovered.
* The mysterious voices and screams throughout the facility abruptly stopped before the session ended.
