The strange glow beneath the ballroom erupts into violence as hidden Children of the Night reveal themselves. The party defeats multiple vampires, uncovers the true purpose of another ritual circle, learns more about the King's Mercy, and receives new leads pointing toward Soul Cairn and the growing influence of the Last Vampire.

---

## 1. Blood Beneath the Ballroom

The glow beneath the ballroom platform proves to be another ritual site.

Combat erupts.

**Party support**

* Seaghda casts **Bless** on Elliot, Exanima, Plagueis, and himself.
* **Aid** is cast on Exanima, Elliot, and Plagueis.

The battle confirms that Children of the Night have infiltrated the gathering.

---

## 2. The Vampires

Multiple vampiric enemies emerge.

### The Woman

One female vampire appears to die.

Instead:

* Her body disintegrates.
* She escapes eastward through cracks in the walls.
* Her destruction appears incomplete.

### The Bone Vampire

A skeletal vampire is removed from the battle.

* Banished using **Maze**.
* Fate remains unknown.

---

## 3. Bram Falls

During the fighting:

* **Bram Blackthorne** is killed.

The Fighters' Guild mourns his apparent death.

Later:

* Bram is successfully restored through revivification.

---

## 4. The Ritual Circle

After the battle, the party examines the ritual site.

Notable observations:

* The circle is built directly into the floor.
* Unlike previous floating circles.
* Resembles the circles found beneath Lamplight and within the corrupted Sunstone chapel.

### Exanima's Artifact

The artifact reacts again.

The response mirrors previous incidents involving:

* Vampire deaths.
* Ritual circles.
* Crown-era magic.

---

## 5. Secrets of the Circle

Seaghda uses **Identify** on the ritual.

The circle serves several functions.

### Transportation

Allows movement between locations.

### Resurrection

Can revive a Child of the Night.

Different materials are required depending on the intended purpose.

### Communion

Can be used to communicate directly with **Nerscylla**.

The discovery suggests the ritual circles form part of a larger vampiric network.

---

## 6. Ashcombe Necklace

Seaghda recovers a necklace from the escaped vampire.

Description:

* Gold necklace.
* Ashcombe markings.
* Amethyst gemstone centerpiece.

Its significance remains unknown.

---

## 7. Keeper's Cleanup

A Keeper of the Last Ember arrives.

Actions:

* Burns the vampire bodies.
* Quietly passes a message to Ansel.
* Appears unusually interested in recent events.

---

## 8. Notes on the King's Mercy

Seaghda shares recovered notes regarding the King's Mercy with House Demerin scholars.

The notes originate from:

* An Elven scholar.
* Approximately eighty years old.

Contents suggest:

* Vampiric dust is required for reforging.
* The blade may have served a different purpose than modern history claims.

The scholars react with both fascination and concern.

---

## 9. History of the Blade

Further discussion reveals additional information.

According to Demerin researchers:

* The King's Mercy predates the first King of Aaldharyn.
* It may originate before the kingdom itself.
* The blade grants immense power.
* The cost is overwhelming bloodlust.

One theory links the blade to the madness of the Mad King.

Another suggests it once granted sufficient power to challenge beings before they ascended.

---

## 10. Missing Piece

The Demerins provide additional schematics.

Important discoveries:

* The sword remains incomplete.
* One shard is still missing.
* A clear diamond-shaped gemstone occupied the final slot.

Without it, the weapon cannot be fully restored.

### Joren's Offer

Record Keeper Joren offers assistance.

*"If you ever go to Achroma, just ask for Joren."*

---

## 11. Soul Cairn Armories

Exanima questions the scholars regarding additional armories.

Information provided:

* Two more armories exist within Soul Cairn.
* Soul Cairn is the largest graveyard in the region.
* Demerin researchers intend to remain there for at least a month.

---

## 12. Erendor's Future

Elliot spends time with members of the Argent Vale.

A gift is provided for Erendor.

### Stabilization Bracers

Specialized bracers designed to:

* Stabilize sanity during transformation.
* Help maintain control while transformed.

The item is difficult to create.

### Purification

The Argent Vale possesses methods capable of purifying corrupted souls.

However:

* The process is dangerous.
* It may be fatal.
* It can only be performed within Argent Vale.

This knowledge remains closely guarded.

---

## 13. Bram's Return

After being revived, Bram discusses death with Elliot.

Bram describes:

* Quiet halls.
* Endless candles.
* A journey beyond life.

He attributes the experience to his connection with the Silent Choir.

The experience appears peaceful rather than frightening.

---

## 14. Devon and Oltheros

Devon asks House Demerin about the ancient maps and symbols connected to his memories.

The scholars identify them.

### Oltheros

A lost kingdom predating modern Aaldharyn.

Characteristics:

* Hawk heraldry.
* Clothing patterns matching Devon's attire.
* Symbols matching the recovered Orins.

Additional discoveries:

* Devon's remembered homeland now lies beneath the Temples of the Ascended.
* The area once gathered immense spiritual power.
* The site may have contributed to the creation of a divine plane.

The revelation deepens the mystery surrounding Devon's origins.

---

## 15. Recognition

The High Marshal formally addresses the party.

Reward:

**800 crowns each**

Given for:

* Defending the gathering.
* Protecting the High Marshal.
* Exposing the vampiric threat.

---

## 16. Ansel's New Mission

Keeper Halbet Rune speaks privately with Ansel.

### The Children of the Night

According to the Keeper:

* Their numbers continue to increase.
* More people are aligning with them.
* Many have gathered within Soulstone.

### Festival of the Dead

Within five days:

* Soul Cairn will host the Festival of the Dead.
* The festival is only thirty to forty years old.
* Relics once belonging to the Last Vampire will be displayed.

The Keeper is deeply concerned.

### Sacred Task

Ansel is instructed to:

* Investigate the relics.
* Determine their purpose.
* Destroy them if necessary.

Complication:

* The High Marshal opposes their destruction.

The Keeper advises caution.

---

## 17. An Unusual Absence

One final detail emerges.

The female member of the Fighters' Guild transformed into a Thrall.

Unlike previous incidents:

* No earthquake occurred.
* No tremor was felt.

This contradicts previous patterns linking Thrall awakenings to seismic disturbances.

The implication remains unclear.
