## At a glance

  ------------ -------------------------------
  **Played**   Notes recorded 2026-08 period
  **Where**    Soul Cairn, Night of Lanterns
  ------------ -------------------------------

The party prepares for the Night of Lanterns while the struggle over the
Pale Ascendant relic intensifies. The Pale White Lantern and Veil become
central to a confrontation involving the High Keeper, Belladonna,
Failsafe, and a mysterious creature.

---

## 1. Nerella Voss

Nerella Voss's identity is confirmed following the previous
confrontation.

Eldros has already departed.

Seaghdha receives the **Crown of Thorns** and **Nightbloom**.

---

## 2. The Night of Lanterns

The party arrives during the Night of Lanterns ceremony.

The festival becomes the setting for another supernatural confrontation.

---

## 3. Failsafe and Corvus

The party encounters:

-   **Failsafe**, Third Fleet Iron Guardian
-   **Corvus**, a changeling associated with Failsafe

Their presence reinforces the continuing involvement of the ancient Iron
Guardians.

---

## 4. The Pale Ascendant Relic

The party encounters the relic associated with the Pale Ascendant.

The **Shroud** becomes central to the ritual.

---

## 5. The High Keeper

**High Keeper Helvic** appears alongside **Belladonna Thornveil**.

Belladonna carries the **Pale White Lantern**.

The relics and ritual begin interacting.

---

## 6. The Shroud

The Shroud is purified.

Its color changes:

**red → white**

The transformation suggests that the relic can be altered by sacred
intervention.

---

## 7. The Creature

A huge creature crashes through a window.

The ceremony erupts into chaos.

Two vampires are seen outside.

The exact identity and purpose of the creature remain unresolved.
