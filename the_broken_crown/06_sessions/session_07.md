# Session 7 — The Ball Beneath the Red Moon

[← Session 6](session_06.md) · [Session ledger](session_ledger.md) · [Session 8 →](session_08.md)

---

## At a glance

|            |                                                        |
| :--------- | :----------------------------------------------------- |
| **Played** | 2–3 May 2026 (notes)                                   |
| **Where**  | Hidden chambers beneath Sunstone → tavern → noble ball |

The party discovers the final ritual chamber beneath Sunstone, rescues Ulric, dismantles a New Blood ritual, and learns troubling truths about Exanima's origins. The sun finally returns, noble houses gather for a grand ball, and rumors spread that vampires may already walk among the kingdom's elite.

---

## 1. The Silent Basement

The party reunites with **Plagueis** and **Exanima** after the underground facility falls silent.

**Erendor**

* Stable but near death.
* Handcuffed to a bedpost for safety.

**Exanima**

* Receives the recovered construction manual from Devon.
* Manual is written in **Old Common**.
* Examines the recovered **Orins**.

**Discovery**

* One coin bears the likeness of a mortal child resembling Exanima.
* Dates to the transition period between older currency and Crown coinage.
* Suggests Exanima predates the modern kingdom.

---

## 2. The Final Ritual Chamber

Following bloodied footprints, the party discovers the final chamber.

Inside:

* **Ulric** bound within an incomplete ritual circle.
* Multiple bodies arranged around a false throne.
* Victims with bloodied eyes.
* One corpse hanging from the ceiling.
* Rows of emptied coffins from **Soul Cairn**.
* Dried blood and ritual basins.
* A pile of skulls.

The room appears heavily desecrated.

---

## 3. Journal of the Bald Man

A journal is recovered from the chamber.

Contents include:

* Notes regarding experimentation on captives.
* Procedures involving transformed bodies.
* Research into vampirism.
* A ritual for contacting **Nerscylla**.
* Instructions for becoming a **New Blood**.

**Seaghda** keeps the journal.

The ritual circle appears to call to him, though unlike previous circles it cannot easily be condensed into a bloodstone.

---

## 4. Dismantling the Circle

Exanima's artifact reacts to the ritual.

**Elliot** identifies the circle as an advanced vampiric ritual and containment structure.

Requirements:

* Multiple living participants.
* Specialized knowledge to dismantle safely.

After roughly twenty minutes:

* Elliot
* Ansel
* Exanima

successfully unravel the ritual.

### Result

The circle collapses inward.

Its magic hardens into a ruby-like construct which is absorbed into Exanima's device.

A new artifact is created.

---

## 5. Ulric Rescued

With the ritual broken, Ulric regains consciousness.

Information provided:

* Captured while traveling toward the Greywarden District.
* Remembers being restrained.
* Explosions occurred nearby.
* Saw the bald man writing in a journal before leaving.

Important revelation:

* The bald man is **not** listed among House Demerin's official representatives.

Ulric appears completely human and unaffected by vampiric transformation.

---

## 6. Shrine of the Pale Shepherd

The party re-examines the shrine room.

Observations:

* Divine energy radiates from the Pale Shepherd statue.
* Red roses adorn the shrine.

Attempting to remove the flowers causes them to wilt immediately upon leaving the chamber.

---

## 7. Return to the Surface

The party emerges from the tavern.

Notable developments:

* It is now approximately 4 p.m.
* The bloody footprints disappear without a trace.
* Ka'el is missing.
* Corvin and Ulric both remain suspicious of the mysterious bald Demerin operative.

Corvin distributes invitations to the upcoming ball.

Additional news:

* The head of the Greywardens will attend.
* Bram Blackthorne has been summoned by House Ravencroft.

---

## 8. Yselle Blackthorne

Ansel is approached by **Yselle Blackthorne**.

She requests the recovered Scarlet Bramble materials.

According to Yselle:

* The Blackthornes believe the artifact may help end the perpetual moon.
* They do not intend to destroy it.
* They will attempt to restore the sun.

In exchange:

* Ansel receives a **Sending Stone**.
* The party receives invitations.
* Three additional guest invitations are granted.

Yselle appears trustworthy and willing to honor her agreements.

---

## 9. Keeper Halbet Rune

While preparing for the ball, Ansel encounters **Keeper Halbet Rune**.

A senior authority within the Church of the Last Ember.

Discussion topics:

* Recent events in Sunstone.
* The voice of the Last Ember.
* Undead activity.
* The state of the kingdom.

Halbet confirms:

* The Cinder's Synod is aware of developments.
* Ansel has not strayed from the path.
* Ansel is approaching the rank of **Warden of Ash**.

### Blessing

Ansel receives:

* 15 temporary hit points until the next long rest.

*"May the Lone Fire guide your path."*

---

## 10. Ka'el Returns

Ka'el eventually returns.

Condition:

* Exhausted.
* Disoriented.
* Missing memories.

His symptoms resemble those observed in Lamplight.

Notable detail:

* He cannot remember the bald man's face.
* Describes the memory as a "silhouette-shaped hole."

**Remove Curse** restores much of his awareness.

Ka'el delivers Ulric's reward:

* 300 crowns.
* A personal note promising future assistance.

Most importantly:

**The sun has returned.**

---

## 11. Record Keeper Jorenfeld

A Fire Genasi bearing House Demerin insignia arrives.

Identifies:

* Exanima.
* His artifacts.
* His unusual behavior.

Observations:

* Exanima is "defective."
* He is creating his own memories.
* He is behaving beyond intended parameters.

Jorenfeld specializes in **Iron Guardian research**.

He later offers:

* Access to Achroma.
* Future study opportunities.
* A communication device as a token of friendship.

---

## 12. Erendor's Recovery

Elliot spends time speaking with Erendor.

Discussion points:

* Gratitude for being spared.
* Fear of encountering Caldrick again.
* Attempts to understand his transformation.

### Controlled Transformation

Erendor attempts a partial shift.

Results:

* Remains mentally aware.
* Gains bestial features.
* Maintains control for approximately thirty minutes.

Afterward:

* Breathing becomes erratic.
* Physical strain increases rapidly.

He successfully reverts.

---

## 13. The Noble Ball

The party attends the gathering of nobles, scholars, military leaders, and guild representatives.

The group uses the event to spread warnings regarding growing vampiric activity.

General concerns emerge:

* Vampire numbers appear to be increasing.
* Nobles may already be compromised.
* Thrall activity may be rising across the kingdom.

---

## 14. Seraph Ashcombe

Bram Blackthorne shares a troubling discovery.

A woman identifying herself as:

**Seraph Ashcombe**

has appeared among the nobility.

Description:

* Red Tiefling.
* Black hair.
* Red eyes.

Investigation reveals:

* Bram does not recognize her.
* Chronicler Maelis Demerin finds no evidence supporting her claim.
* No records connect her to House Ashcombe.

Her identity remains unknown.

---

## 15. New Contacts

### Fighters' Guild

An all-female delegation attends the ball.

Among them:

* Loretta of House Valemyr.

### Record Keeper Jorenfeld

Further discussions reveal:

* Research focus on Iron Guardians.
* Interest in Exanima.
* Desire to bring Exanima to Achroma for study.

### Growing Threat

Representatives report:

* Increasing vampire activity.
* A vampire matching Eldros's description is being hunted.
* Heavy earthquakes may indicate another Thrall awakening.
* Vampires can conceal themselves even from holy scrutiny.

---

## 16. A Glow Beneath the Floor

Toward the end of the evening, the party notices:

* A faint glow beneath the ballroom platform.

The attending Martials appear unaware of it.

At the same time:

* Erendor requests to return to the tavern as a precaution.

The session ends with the source of the glow still unexplained.

* The bald vampire's journal contains a ritual for creating New Bloods.
* Ulric was rescued before transformation could be completed.
* Exanima absorbed another ritual-derived artifact.
* The mysterious bald man is not an official Demerin representative.
* Ka'el suffered memory manipulation similar to Lamplight's victims.
* The sun finally returns after days of darkness.
* Exanima may be developing unintended memories and behavior.
* Erendor can partially control his lycanthropy.
* Seraph Ashcombe has no documented connection to House Ashcombe.
* Vampire numbers are increasing throughout the kingdom.
