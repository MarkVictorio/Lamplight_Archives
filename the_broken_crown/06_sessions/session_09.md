The aftermath of the noble ball sends the party deeper into the
mysteries surrounding vampires, the Soul Cairn, and the forgotten
kingdom of Oltheros. Exanima is taken for further work, Julianos reveals
another side of vampirism, and Devon begins recovering memories of a
life that predates Aaldharyn.

---

## 1. Evacuation and Separation

The party deals with the aftermath of the ballroom incident.

-   Soulstone begins responding to the threat.
-   Exanima is taken by House Demerin for further upgrades and
    examination.
-   The party continues investigating the growing vampire activity.
-   The Festival of the Dead becomes increasingly important to the
    investigation.

---

## 2. Julianos

A strange figure named **Julianos** approaches the party.

He appears connected to the vampire incidents and claims to want a
reversal of his condition.

The party attempts to determine whether he can be restored.

### Ansel's Intervention

Ansel attempts to use **Destroy Undead**.

The attempt fails.

Julianos reacts violently and is ultimately killed.

His behavior reinforces the growing distinction between ordinary undead,
Thralls, and the newer forms of vampirism appearing throughout the
kingdom.

---

## 3. The Military Note

The party recovers:

-   **500 Orins**
-   A military note referencing **Oltheros**
-   A reference to **General Sten Rowe**

The note is especially significant to Devon.

---

## 4. The Cloak Buttons

The recovered military material contains details that Devon recognizes.

The cloak buttons associated with the note match those connected to
Devon's own clothing and memories.

This suggests that Devon's connection to Oltheros is more than a vague
resemblance.

---

## 5. Lantern Road

The party travels along Lantern Road during the Festival of the Dead.

The road is filled with:

-   lanterns
-   mourners
-   travelers
-   religious activity
-   rumors surrounding the recent vampire attacks

The celebration provides a sharp contrast to the supernatural events
unfolding beneath it.

---

## 6. Evander and Leonora

The party encounters **Evander** and **Leonora**.

Evander's presence continues to raise questions regarding the Order of
the Waning Veil.

The party does not yet know the full extent of his involvement.

---

## 7. The Soul Fountain

The party reaches the **Soul Fountain**.

The site contains an enormous concentration of spiritual power.

Its purpose appears connected to:

-   the dead
-   the Soul Cairn
-   the Ascended
-   the spiritual structure of the world

The scale of the power suggests that the site may be more than a
religious monument.

---

## 8. Devon Remembers

Devon's contact with the Soul Fountain triggers powerful memories.

He recalls:

-   marble and limestone structures
-   a large fountain
-   an acropolis
-   a childhood home
-   a civilization that no longer exists

The memories strongly resemble the ancient kingdom later identified as
**Oltheros**.

---

## 9. The Skeletal Thrall

A skeletal Thrall appears within the market.

Its presence demonstrates that the phenomenon is spreading beyond
isolated ritual sites.

The party continues to encounter evidence that the old system
surrounding Thralls is becoming unstable.

---

## 10. Seaghdha and the Crown

Seaghdha attunes to the **Crown of Scarlet Bramble**.

The artifact's connection to him deepens.

Its significance remains tied to the older magical and religious history
surrounding the Scarlet Bramble.
